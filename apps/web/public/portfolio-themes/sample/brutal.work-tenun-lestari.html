Tenun Lestari identity

Identity, packaging and a pop-up shop for a cooperative of 120 weavers.

My role Lead brand designerYear 2024
  • Identity
  • Packaging
  • Pop-up

Problem

A cooperative of 120 weavers in Karangasem sells the finest cloth, yet at fairs it stood out less than city brands. Packaging and signs were improvised.

My role

  • Researched motifs with 12 senior weavers.
  • Designed the logo, colour system, cloth labels and boxes.
  • Directed the pop-up shop for the Jakarta fair.

Process

The logo comes from the base pattern of gringsing cloth, simplified to four shapes. Every weaver gets a label with her name and village.

The boxes use recycled card and one print colour, so each costs under IDR 4,000.

Buyers want to know who wove their cloth. That label is now the most photographed part.
