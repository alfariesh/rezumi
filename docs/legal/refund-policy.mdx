## Eligibility

- **First purchase:** full refund requested within 7 × 24 hours of verified payment, with at most 10 Rezumi AI credits
  used from that purchase. Plus with no credit usage meets this threshold.
- **Duplicate payments:** valid payments normally add access time. Submit related order numbers to refund unwanted excess payments after verification.
- **Technical failure:** payment debited without access, or service that does not match the offer and cannot be restored,
  is reviewed for remediation or refund. The promotional 7-day/10-credit threshold does not remove these rights.
- Other requests are assessed on facts and law; there is no blanket no-refund policy.

## Returning funds

Supported QRIS/e-wallet refunds are submitted through Midtrans to the original payment source. Some cross-app QRIS transactions
have a submission window of about **7 days from settlement**; request promptly. This technical window does not shorten customer refund rights.
If it has elapsed or the method is unsupported, including virtual accounts, Rezumi arranges a **manual transfer** after recipient verification.
Bank details are requested through support only for the transfer; Rezumi does not store banking/card credentials.
Transfer evidence may retain necessary recipient information for accounting obligations.

Receipt timing depends on banks/providers. Rezumi supplies submission evidence, an estimate on approval and updates on delays;
funds are not guaranteed to arrive within 7 days. A full refund returns the purchase amount without deducting Rezumi/Midtrans processing fees.

## Access and credits

On approval, the refunded transaction's access period is revoked and the queue recalculated; other purchases retain their time.
Remaining credits from the refunded purchase are revoked. Used credits do not become a cash debt; eligibility and possible abuse
are assessed during review. Other purchases' credits remain. Documents are never deleted or locked and remain editable,
shareable and downloadable as PDF under Free or any remaining paid access. A notice explains adjustments and confirms processing.
Mandatory legal rights remain available.
