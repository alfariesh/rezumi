The [Indonesian refund policy](/legal/kebijakan-pengembalian-dana) governs. Effective date: **12 November 2026**.
See [Contact & complaints](/legal/contact) for seller details and the [Terms](/legal/terms-of-service).

## Eligibility

- **First charge of a card subscription:** full refund requested within 7 × 24 hours of the verified first charge, with at most
  10 Rezumi AI credits used from it. Plus with no credit usage meets this threshold. For Plus with a free trial, the first charge
  is the one made when the 7 free days end; cancelling during the trial charges nothing, so there is nothing to refund.
- **QRIS plan payments:** each QRIS plan payment can be fully refunded when requested within 7 × 24 hours of that payment's
  verification, with at most 10 Rezumi AI credits used from that plan. Plus with no credit usage meets this threshold.
- **Duplicate payments:** an account has one active card subscription. If two charges still cover the same period, or the same
  purchase is paid twice by QRIS, send your account email and the dates so the excess payment is refunded after verification.
- **Cancelling a subscription:** cancelling a card subscription stops the next renewal; the running period is not refunded
  automatically unless it meets the conditions above or statutory rights apply. A QRIS plan doesn't renew, so there is nothing to
  cancel; the rest of its time is not refunded automatically either, with the same exceptions.
- **Technical failure:** payment debited without access, or service that does not match the offer and cannot be restored,
  is reviewed for remediation or refund. The promotional 7-day/10-credit threshold does not remove these rights.
- Other requests are assessed on facts and law; there is no blanket no-refund policy.

## Requests and deadlines

Send account email, the Dodo Payments invoice or receipt number (card) or the order number starting with **RZ-** from your
payment receipt (QRIS), date, amount, reason and relevant evidence to **hi@rezumi.app** or complaint phone **08125800097**. For a
QRIS refund, also send the account to receive it: holder name, bank or e-wallet name, and account or e-wallet number. Never send
passwords, OTPs, PINs or card numbers.
Rezumi checks server payment/credit records, requests necessary details and sends a reasoned decision.
Service hours: **Monday–Friday, 09:00–17:00 WIB (UTC+7)**. First response within **3 business days**.
Resolution/refund decision deadline: **2 × 24 hours of receiving complete details**.
Unresolved complaints may be escalated through [Contact & complaints](/legal/contact).

## Returning funds

**Card subscriptions.** Subscriptions are sold by **Dodo Payments, Inc.** as the merchant of record. Approved refunds are
submitted by Rezumi through Dodo Payments and returned to the **original payment method** (card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or another
method used), including the tax paid with it. For card refunds, Rezumi never asks for bank or card details. If a payment is disputed
through the bank or card issuer (a chargeback) and the dispute is decided for the buyer, the credits from that payment are taken
back and the subscription ends. Receipt timing depends on the bank or card issuer, usually a few business days.

**QRIS plans.** QRIS plans are sold directly by **Salman Alfariesh — Rezumi**; the payment is processed by SumoPod (PT Koding Kreasi
Indonesia), which can't return money to the original QRIS payment. Approved refunds are therefore **sent manually** by the operator,
by bank transfer or e-wallet, to the account you name. The money is sent within the resolution deadline above, counted from
receiving complete details including the receiving account; when it arrives depends on the receiving bank or e-wallet provider.

Rezumi confirms when a refund is submitted or the money is sent, and updates you on delays. A full refund returns the purchase
amount without deducting processing or transfer fees from the customer.

## Access and credits

A refund does not end a card subscription on its own; if you don't want to continue, the subscription ends together with the refund
and paid access stops. A full refund of a QRIS payment ends that QRIS plan at once, or removes it from the queue if it hasn't
started; QRIS plans queued after it move up. Access granted otherwise remains.
Remaining credits from the refunded purchase are revoked. Used credits do not become a cash debt; eligibility and possible abuse
are assessed during review. Other purchases' credits remain. Documents are never deleted or locked and remain editable,
shareable and downloadable as PDF under Free or any remaining paid access. A notice explains adjustments and confirms processing.
Mandatory legal rights remain available.
