## Eligibility

- **First purchase:** full refund requested within 7 × 24 hours of verified payment, with at most 10 Rezumi AI credits
  used from that purchase. Plus with no credit usage meets this threshold.
- **Duplicate payments:** an account has one active subscription. If two charges still cover the same period, send your account
  email and the dates so the excess charge is refunded after verification.
- **Cancelling a subscription:** cancelling stops the next renewal; the running period is not refunded automatically unless it
  meets the conditions above or statutory rights apply.
- **Technical failure:** payment debited without access, or service that does not match the offer and cannot be restored,
  is reviewed for remediation or refund. The promotional 7-day/10-credit threshold does not remove these rights.
- Other requests are assessed on facts and law; there is no blanket no-refund policy.

## Returning funds

Subscriptions are sold by **Polar Software, Inc.** as the merchant of record. Approved refunds are submitted by Rezumi through Polar
and returned to the **original payment method** (card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or another method used), including the tax paid with it.
Rezumi never asks for bank or card details.

Polar may also refund a purchase at its own discretion up to 60 days after it to prevent a payment dispute; the subscription then
ends as well. Receipt timing depends on the bank or card issuer, usually a few business days. Rezumi confirms submission and updates
you on delays. A full refund returns the purchase amount without deducting processing fees from the customer.

## Access and credits

A refund does not end a subscription on its own; if you don't want to continue, the subscription ends together with the refund
and paid access stops. Access granted otherwise remains.
Remaining credits from the refunded purchase are revoked. Used credits do not become a cash debt; eligibility and possible abuse
are assessed during review. Other purchases' credits remain. Documents are never deleted or locked and remain editable,
shareable and downloadable as PDF under Free or any remaining paid access. A notice explains adjustments and confirms processing.
Mandatory legal rights remain available.
