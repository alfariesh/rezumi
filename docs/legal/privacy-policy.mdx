## Mobile and permissions

Login tokens use OS secure storage. Private app storage holds preferences, drafts and cached documents/fonts/images/exports.
Photos use the system picker; camera is optional for profile photos, microphone for recorded interview practice.
Transcription uploads audio through the server to the chosen provider; generated speech sends text to a speech provider.
These are not local speech processing. Keep audio controls local copies, not provider retention. Manage camera/microphone/notification permissions in OS settings.
Reminders use local notifications; the app does not register for remote push.

Mobile requests data consent before AI operations; declining blocks them. Enabling a career schedule authorizes recurring processing
under its settings until disabled. AI/shared-resume reports send only chosen content, category, explanation, account identifier and review timestamps
to the operator, not AI providers. Reports are included in account export and deleted with the account or earlier after review.
Author blocks are local per account/server; they do not remove website content or affect other devices.

## Web portfolios

This section applies once portfolios launch.

| Data                                                                                      | Purpose                                 | Basis                                               |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Portfolio content, pictures, PDFs and location written by the user                        | Showing the portfolio to visitors       | Contract; the user's choice to publish              |
| Contact form messages: name, email, message                                               | Passing messages to the owner           | Contract and visitors' legitimate interests         |
| Aggregate statistics: visits, clicks, sources, countries, device types, referring domains | Statistics for the portfolio's owner    | Proportionate legitimate interests                  |
| Reports: reason, details, reporter's email if given                                       | Content moderation and enforcing rights | Legal obligations and security legitimate interests |
| Moderation records: who acted, when and why                                               | Accountability and handling appeals     | Legitimate interests and legal obligations          |

- **Content users publish** is visible to anyone. Pictures are compressed in the browser, which removes photo location data (EXIF). PDFs are published as they are, including details saved inside the
  file such as the author's name.
- **Statistics without tracking cookies.** Visits and clicks are told apart with a hash of IP address, browser and language for one hour; the raw IP address is not stored. Country, device type and
  referring domain breakdowns are kept as aggregates in **Cloudflare Analytics Engine** for about 3 months.
- **Contact form.** Messages are kept for **90 days** and then deleted automatically; the portfolio's owner can delete them sooner. The owner sees the sender's email so they can reply.
  **Cloudflare Turnstile** prevents spam on the contact and report forms and processes visitors' technical data as a third party.
- **Reports.** Reports are read only by the Rezumi team. Who reported and what they wrote are not shown to the portfolio's owner and are not in their data export, which holds only the reason, status and time.
  A reporter's email is deleted 90 days after the report is handled. Reports are deleted with the reported portfolio or account. Moderation records are kept after an account is deleted, without naming the account.
- **Videos** from YouTube and Vimeo load only after a visitor clicks play; until then no request goes to those services.
- **Pasted links.** When a user pastes a link, Rezumi's server reads that page's title and picture without sending any user data. If the user approves the picture, a copy is stored with their portfolio files.
- **Location and maps.** Location is shown at city level by default, and a user can choose not to show one. Map pictures are made in the editor, so opening a portfolio sends no request to a map provider.
  While choosing a location in the editor, the user's browser loads map data from OpenFreeMap.
- **Custom domains.** A connected domain name is processed by Cloudflare (Cloudflare for SaaS) for certificates and routing.
- **Export and deletion.** The account export holds the portfolio, its file list, messages, and reports as described above. Deleting the account deletes everything, including files and the Cloudflare custom hostname.

## Retention and deletion

Account data, documents, uploads and versions remain as needed until deletion. Trash is permanently removed after 30 days unless restored/deleted sooner.
App consents/tokens remain until revoked, expired or account deletion. Delete through **Settings → Account → Delete account**;
mobile opens server browser confirmation and may require login. Uninstalling does not delete an account. Clean exported files/device caches separately.

On deletion, identity links in payment records are removed/anonymized; records must not be called anonymous if re-identification remains possible.
Non-identifying accounting records are planned for **10 years**.
If law requires identifiable evidence, retain only the necessary portion separately with restricted access and disclose the basis and duration.
Law or disputes may delay relevant deletion with an explanation. Logs and support tickets are kept for at most **1 year**. AI usage records follow the accounting records above. The database restore history is kept for **6 hours**. No promise of instant erasure from every backup/provider; completion and residual retention are explained.

## Rights

You may request processing information, access/copies, correction, deletion/destruction, withdrawal of consent, cessation/restriction,
portability subject to legal conditions, objection to significant automated decisions and complaints/compensation.
Rezumi does not promise automated hiring decisions. Account export/editing are available in-app; email for other requests.
Identity checks are proportionate. Withdrawal affects future processing; legal duties may still require limited retention.
Access, correction, stopping processing on consent withdrawal and restriction are handled within **3 × 24 hours** where that statutory deadline applies;
other rights follow their specific deadlines. Legal restrictions are explained with an objection route.

## Security, incidents and international transfers

We use access controls, HTTPS, storage separation and credential protection; no system is risk-free.
A personal data protection failure is notified in writing to affected people and the competent institution within **3 × 24 hours** under the PDP Law,
including affected data, timing/circumstances and response measures; public notice is given where required.

Cloudflare, Neon, email and AI providers may process data outside Indonesia. Before transfer, assess equivalent protection;
otherwise ensure adequate binding safeguards, and if those conditions cannot be met obtain consent as required by law.
Processing locations: Singapore (Neon), the United States (Dodo Payments, Anthropic), India (Dodo Payments) and Cloudflare's global network. Transfers follow the data processing agreement with each provider.
