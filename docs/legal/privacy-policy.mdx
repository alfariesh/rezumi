## Mobile and permissions

Login tokens use OS secure storage. Private app storage holds preferences, drafts and cached documents/fonts/images/exports.
Photos use the system picker; camera is optional for profile photos, microphone for recorded interview practice.
Transcription uploads audio through the server to the chosen provider; generated speech sends text to a speech provider.
These are not local speech processing. Keep audio controls local copies, not provider retention. Manage camera/microphone/notification permissions in OS settings.
Reminders use local notifications; the app does not register for remote push.

Mobile requests data consent before AI operations; declining blocks them. Enabling a career schedule authorizes recurring processing
under its settings until disabled. AI/shared-resume reports send only chosen content, category, explanation, account identifier and review timestamps
to the operator, not AI providers. Reports are included in account export and deleted with the account or earlier after review.
Author blocks are local per account/server; they do not remove website content or affect other devices.

## Retention and deletion

Account data, documents, uploads and versions remain as needed until deletion. Trash is permanently removed after 30 days unless restored/deleted sooner.
App consents/tokens remain until revoked, expired or account deletion. Delete through **Settings → Account → Delete account**;
mobile opens server browser confirmation and may require login. Uninstalling does not delete an account. Clean exported files/device caches separately.

On deletion, identity links in payment records are removed/anonymized; records must not be called anonymous if re-identification remains possible.

## Rights

You may request processing information, access/copies, correction, deletion/destruction, withdrawal of consent, cessation/restriction,
portability subject to legal conditions, objection to significant automated decisions and complaints/compensation.
Rezumi does not promise automated hiring decisions. Account export/editing are available in-app; email for other requests.
Identity checks are proportionate. Withdrawal affects future processing; legal duties may still require limited retention.
Access, correction, stopping processing on consent withdrawal and restriction are handled within **3 × 24 hours** where that statutory deadline applies;
other rights follow their specific deadlines. Legal restrictions are explained with an objection route.

## Security, incidents and international transfers

We use access controls, HTTPS, storage separation and credential protection; no system is risk-free.
A personal data protection failure is notified in writing to affected people and the competent institution within **3 × 24 hours** under the PDP Law,
including affected data, timing/circumstances and response measures; public notice is given where required.

Cloudflare, Neon, email and AI providers may process data outside Indonesia. Before transfer, assess equivalent protection;
otherwise ensure adequate binding safeguards, and if those conditions cannot be met obtain consent as required by law.
