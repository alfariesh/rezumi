## Mobile and permissions

Login tokens use OS secure storage. Private app storage holds preferences, drafts and cached documents/fonts/images/exports.
Photos use the system picker; camera is optional for profile photos, microphone for recorded interview practice.
Transcription uploads audio through the server to the chosen provider; generated speech sends text to a speech provider.
These are not local speech processing. Keep audio controls local copies, not provider retention. Manage camera/microphone/notification permissions in OS settings.
Reminders use local notifications; the app does not register for remote push.

Mobile requests data consent before AI operations; declining blocks them. Enabling a career schedule authorizes recurring processing
under its settings until disabled. AI/shared-resume reports send only chosen content, category, explanation, account identifier and review timestamps
to the operator, not AI providers. Reports are included in account export and deleted with the account or earlier after review.
Author blocks are local per account/server; they do not remove website content or affect other devices.

## Browser extension

This section covers the Rezumi extension for Chrome and Microsoft Edge (desktop). The extension only saves job postings from your browser to the applications in your Rezumi account and helps you prepare them. Everything is stored in your Rezumi account; the extension has no database of its own.

| Data                                                                                                                                              | Purpose                                                  | Basis                                            |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Address, title, text (up to 20,000 characters), the page's structured job data and the text you select, from a job page you ask it to read        | Filling in and saving the posting to your applications   | Contract; your request                           |
| Sign-in tokens, and the account name and email shown in the panel                                                                                 | Signing in and showing which account is connected        | Contract                                         |
| A page report you send: the page address, site, extension version and names of the fields that failed to read (no page content)                   | Fixing the job site readers                              | Proportionate legitimate interests; your request |
| In your browser: settings, per-tab form drafts, the list of saved posting keys, the list of due follow-ups, and a note of reminders already shown | The "Saved" mark, the icon's count, reminders and drafts | Contract                                         |

- **When it reads a page.** Only when you ask it to: opening the panel on a job page, pressing Save, the right-click menu or the keyboard shortcut. On LinkedIn, Indeed, JobStreet and Glints it also reads the address of the page you are on, inside your browser, to show the "Saved" mark; that address is not sent to us. On any other site it reads nothing until you click it. It never presses a button on a job site and never collects data on its own.
- **What goes to the server.** When the panel is open on a job page, that page's text is sent to the Rezumi server to fill in the fields (role, company, location and so on). The server does not keep it unless you save the posting. Saving creates an application in your account with what you reviewed and edited. Every request carries the extension version and your device's time zone.
- **Optional AI.** "Read with AI" and the AI match score send the posting text (and, for the score, a summary of your resume) to the AI provider named under Recipients, at the credit cost shown. The switch is off by default.
- **The "Saved" mark.** The extension downloads the list of posting keys you have already saved (up to 5,000) and matches them in your browser. The addresses of the pages you visit are not sent for this.
- **Contacts and logos.** An email address or phone number written in a posting's description is only suggested, and saved only if you tick it. People's profiles on job sites are not read. A company logo is downloaded by the Rezumi server from a public address on the page and copied to Rezumi's storage; your browser is not asked to load it from the job site.
- **Sign-in.** The extension signs in with OAuth (PKCE), without copying API keys. The access token is kept briefly in the browser; the refresh token and the account's name and email stay in the extension's local storage until you sign out. Removing the extension deletes that storage from the browser but does not revoke its access on the server: revoke it under **Settings → Connected applications**.
- **Report this page.** Sent only when you click it. Reports are summarised in an email to the operator, kept for at most 30 days, included in the account data export and deleted with the account.
- **Desktop notifications.** Optional and off by default; the browser asks for permission when you turn them on. Reminders are worked out on your device from the follow-up dates you set; no third-party push service is involved.
- **The page after removal.** When the extension is removed, the browser opens a short page on rezumi.app. Its address carries only the language and the extension version, nothing about you, and the page asks for no data.
- **What it does not do.** It loads no code from outside, uses no third-party trackers or analytics, does not sell data and does not use it for advertising or credit decisions.
- **Limited Use.** Use of information received through browser APIs follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements: data is used only for the extension's single purpose (saving and preparing job postings), is not transferred to third parties beyond what that feature needs, is not read by people except to answer your support request, for security, or as the law requires, and is not used or transferred for advertising.

## Web portfolios

This section applies once portfolios launch.

| Data                                                                                      | Purpose                                           | Basis                                               |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Portfolio content, pictures, PDFs and location written by the user                        | Showing the portfolio to visitors                 | Contract; the user's choice to publish              |
| Portfolio password hash and portfolio access cookie                                       | Limiting access to a portfolio its owner protects | Contract; the portfolio owner's choice              |
| Contact form messages: name, email, message                                               | Passing messages to the owner                     | Contract and visitors' legitimate interests         |
| Aggregate statistics: visits, clicks, sources, countries, device types, referring domains | Statistics for the portfolio's owner              | Proportionate legitimate interests                  |
| Reports: reason, details, reporter's email if given                                       | Content moderation and enforcing rights           | Legal obligations and security legitimate interests |
| Moderation records: who acted, when and why                                               | Accountability and handling appeals               | Legitimate interests and legal obligations          |

- **Content users publish** is visible to anyone. Pictures are compressed in the browser, which removes photo location data (EXIF). PDFs are published as they are, including details saved inside the
  file such as the author's name.
- **Statistics without tracking cookies.** Visits and clicks are told apart with a hash of IP address, browser and language for one hour; the raw IP address is not stored. Country, device type and
  referring domain breakdowns are kept as aggregates in **Cloudflare Analytics Engine** for about 3 months.
  Statistics also count the work pages opened, and when a visitor arrives through the QR code on the owner's business card (its address carries `utm_source=qr`) the source is recorded as "QR".
- **Contact form.** Messages are kept for **90 days** and then deleted automatically; the portfolio's owner can delete them sooner. The owner sees the sender's email so they can reply.
  **Cloudflare Turnstile** prevents spam on the contact and report forms and processes visitors' technical data as a third party.
- **Reports.** Reports are read only by the Rezumi team. Who reported and what they wrote are not shown to the portfolio's owner and are not in their data export, which holds only the reason, status and time.
  A reporter's email is deleted 90 days after the report is handled. Reports are deleted with the reported portfolio or account. Moderation records are kept after an account is deleted, without naming the account.
- **Videos** from YouTube and Vimeo load only after a visitor clicks play; until then no request goes to those services.
- **Embeds.** Content from Figma, Loom, Spotify, SoundCloud and Google Slides loads only after a visitor presses its button; until then no request goes to those services (the preview
  picture is a copy stored with the portfolio). After the press, the visitor's browser loads the content straight from that service, which may receive the visitor's IP address and browser
  data, may set or read its own cookies, and processes them under its own privacy policy as a third party. A note under the button says "Loads content from …".
- **Portfolio password.** A portfolio's owner can protect it with one password. The password is stored only as a hash, never as plain text, and is not written to logs or data exports.
  A visitor who enters the right password receives an access cookie that lasts 12 hours in that browser (only a signed portfolio marker and expiry, no personal data), and the portfolio file
  addresses (pictures, PDFs) they are given carry a token that lasts 12 to 24 hours. Wrong password attempts are limited by the visitor's IP address (an IPv6 address by its /64 block) and by
  portfolio; those counts are kept only briefly and never hold the password tried. While a portfolio has a password, its content isn't shown to visitors without it, isn't indexed by
  search engines and isn't in the sitemap. Changing or removing the password ends existing access.
- **Save contact.** A portfolio page can offer a Save contact button that downloads a contact card (vCard) to the visitor's device. The card is made when it's downloaded, from what the page
  already shows: name, headline, portfolio address, and email, WhatsApp number, phone, website, profiles and city only when the owner shows them; no photo and no street address. Rezumi
  stores no visitor data for this; downloads are only counted as aggregate numbers.
- **Pasted links.** When a user pastes a link, Rezumi's server reads that page's title and picture without sending any user data. If the user approves the picture, a copy is stored with their portfolio files.
- **Location and maps.** Location is shown at city level by default, and a user can choose not to show one. Map pictures are made in the editor, so opening a portfolio sends no request to a map provider.
  While choosing a location in the editor, the user's browser loads map data from OpenFreeMap.
- **Custom domains.** A connected domain name is processed by Cloudflare (Cloudflare for SaaS) for certificates and routing.
- **Export and deletion.** The account export holds the portfolio, its file list, messages, and reports as described above. Deleting the account deletes everything, including files and the Cloudflare custom hostname.

## Retention and deletion

Account data, documents, uploads and versions remain as needed until deletion. Trash is permanently removed after 30 days unless restored/deleted sooner.
App consents/tokens remain until revoked, expired or account deletion. Delete through **Settings → Account → Delete account**;
mobile opens server browser confirmation and may require login. Uninstalling does not delete an account. Clean exported files/device caches separately.

On deletion, identity links in payment records are removed/anonymized; records must not be called anonymous if re-identification remains possible.
Non-identifying accounting records are planned for **10 years**.
If law requires identifiable evidence, retain only the necessary portion separately with restricted access and disclose the basis and duration.
Law or disputes may delay relevant deletion with an explanation. Logs and support tickets are kept for at most **1 year**. AI usage records follow the accounting records above. The database restore history is kept for **6 hours**. No promise of instant erasure from every backup/provider; completion and residual retention are explained.

## Security, incidents and international transfers

We use access controls, HTTPS, storage separation and credential protection; no system is risk-free.
A personal data protection failure is notified in writing to affected people and the competent institution within **3 × 24 hours** under the PDP Law,
including affected data, timing/circumstances and response measures; public notice is given where required.

Cloudflare, Neon, email and AI providers may process data outside Indonesia. Before transfer, assess equivalent protection;
otherwise ensure adequate binding safeguards, and if those conditions cannot be met obtain consent as required by law.
Processing locations: Singapore (Neon), the United States (Dodo Payments, Anthropic, and GitHub when you add a GitHub username), India (Dodo Payments) and Cloudflare's global network. Transfers follow the data processing agreement with each provider.
