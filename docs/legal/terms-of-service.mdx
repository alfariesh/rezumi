## Payment and delivery

Paid plans are bought on the website; there are no in-app purchases. The way to pay is chosen automatically by the country you
visit from (based on your IP address): visitors from Indonesia see QRIS in Rupiah first, with a card in US dollars as the other
choice; visitors from other countries pay by card. An account with a QRIS plan running or waiting keeps paying by QRIS wherever it
is used from, and an account with a live card subscription keeps managing it at Dodo Payments.

**Card.** Subscriptions are bought through the checkout of **Dodo Payments** (dodopayments.com). Dodo Payments, Inc. is the
merchant of record and reseller: it charges the payment, issues invoices and receipts, calculates and remits sales tax (VAT, GST,
sales tax, including Indonesian VAT on foreign digital services where it applies), and handles payment disputes. Purchases are also
subject to the buyer terms of Dodo Payments shown at checkout. Available methods are shown at checkout: debit/credit cards, Apple
Pay, Google Pay and other methods Dodo Payments supports in your country.

Card subscription prices are in US dollars and are **before tax**. Dodo Payments calculates the tax that applies where you are (for
example VAT in Indonesia) and adds it; the total is shown at checkout before you pay, on every renewal charge and on the invoice. A
running subscription keeps its price; price changes apply to new subscriptions only.

**QRIS (Indonesia only).** QRIS plans are sold directly by **Salman Alfariesh — Rezumi** (an individual). The payment is processed
by **SumoPod** (sumopod.com), operated by PT Koding Kreasi Indonesia, and can be made with any Indonesian bank or e-wallet app that
supports QRIS (for example GoPay, OVO, DANA, ShopeePay or mobile banking). The payment page (pay.sumopod.com) stays open for 1 hour.
Rupiah prices are **final**: the seller is an individual who is not registered for VAT (not a PKP, _Pengusaha Kena Pajak_), so no
VAT (PPN) is added. Rezumi emails a **payment receipt** and shows it, ready to print, in the payment history on the Plan page. This
receipt is **not a tax invoice** (_faktur pajak_).

Access starts only after **server verification** of the payment, not when checkout opens: card payments are verified with Dodo
Payments, and QRIS payments when Rezumi's server receives SumoPod's confirmation (usually within seconds). An unpaid checkout can be
continued until it expires. Contact support with your account email (for QRIS, also the order number starting with RZ-) if money was
taken but access is missing.

## Subscriptions, renewals and plan changes

Plus and Pro AI paid by card are **subscriptions that renew automatically** every month or every year (as chosen), charging the
saved payment method, until cancelled. Dodo Payments emails a reminder before renewals and a receipt for each charge. **You can cancel at any
time** from the Plan page or the billing portal of Dodo Payments; access continues until the end of the period you paid for, and nothing more is charged.

**Plus free trial.** An account's first Plus subscription by card (monthly or yearly) starts with **7 free days**. Dodo Payments takes a
payment method at checkout but charges nothing during the trial, and sends a reminder before the first charge. Unless cancelled before the
trial ends, the first charge is made then and the subscription continues as usual; cancelled during the trial, nothing is charged.
The trial is once per person: an account that has subscribed or paid for a plan before, including by QRIS, doesn't get it again,
and Dodo Payments may refuse a trial to a buyer who has used one. Upgrading to Pro AI or switching to yearly billing during the
trial ends it and charges at once. Pro AI has no trial.

For card subscriptions, upgrades (Plus to Pro AI, or monthly to yearly) apply at once; the difference for the rest of the current period is charged then
(proration calculated by Dodo Payments; the renewal date stays, except a move to yearly, which starts a new yearly period with the
rest of the monthly one credited). Downgrades (Pro AI to Plus, or yearly to monthly) apply at the next renewal. If a renewal charge
fails, Dodo Payments may retry; paid access is paused until the payment method is updated in the billing portal, and the
subscription ends if payment never succeeds. One active subscription per account.

**QRIS plans.** A QRIS plan is a **prepaid pass** that lasts 30 or 365 days and **does not renew automatically**; nothing is charged
later. Rezumi emails a reminder 3 days before it ends. Paying again at any time adds the days **after** the current plan, so no day
is lost. Buying Pro AI while on Plus starts Pro AI at once; the rest of the Plus time is paused and continues after Pro AI ends.
Buying a lower plan while on a higher one queues it after the higher one. QRIS plans have no free trial, and discount codes can't
be used for QRIS payments yet.

When a subscription, a QRIS plan or other paid access ends, the account returns to Free. Documents remain available to open, edit, share and download
as PDF, including saved premium templates, tool logos, CSS and fonts. Public links remain live. Limits restrict creating, importing,
duplicating, tailoring, copying for a job and restoring from Trash when full. Guest documents transferred at registration are exempt.
Selecting premium templates, turning on tool logos, changing CSS/fonts, making new Word/ODT exports and third-party API/MCP require an
active paid plan. Downloaded files remain yours.

Once portfolios launch, a portfolio is also never deleted when a plan ends: the theme and effect in use keep showing but can't be changed
to another paid one, the Made with Rezumi badge returns, and works over 6 and files over the quota keep showing but can't be added to.
Connecting your own domain, for a portfolio or a resume, requires an active paid plan; a connected domain stops 7 days after the plan ends,
points to `rezumi.app/<username>`, and works again once a paid plan is active again.

Deleting your account ends a card subscription and any QRIS plan at once, without an automatic refund; Dodo Payments keeps its
card transaction records for its legal and bookkeeping obligations as the seller.

Failed operations without usable results return credits; double clicks and network retries must not double-charge.
BYOK never consumes credits; the AI source never switches silently. Voice practice, scheduled discovery and paid web tools are outside
launch credits; your own connections remain available.

## Cancellation and refunds

Cancelling a card subscription stops the next renewal; the period already running is not refunded automatically. The first charge
of a card subscription qualifies for a full refund when requested within 7 days of verified payment and no more than 10 credits from
it were used. For Plus with a free trial, the first charge is the one made when the trial ends; the trial itself is free. Each QRIS
plan payment qualifies for a full refund on the same terms: requested within 7 days of verified payment, with no more than 10 credits
from that plan used. Duplicate payments and technical failures are reviewed separately.

Card subscription refunds go through Dodo Payments to the original payment method. SumoPod can't return money to the original QRIS
payment, so QRIS plan refunds are **sent manually** by the operator, by bank transfer or e-wallet, to an account you name; request
one through hi@rezumi.app with the order number (starting with RZ-). A full refund ends that QRIS plan (plans queued after it move up)
and takes back its remaining credits. See the [Refund policy](/legal/refund-policy) for access/credit adjustments. Statutory
remedies for nonconforming service remain available.
