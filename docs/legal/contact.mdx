## Procedure

1. Email with subject “Rezumi complaint”, “Rezumi refund” or “Personal data rights”. Include account email,
   issue, event time and order number for payments. Never send passwords, OTPs, PINs or unnecessary identity data.
2. Support acknowledges receipt, provides a complaint reference and requests further evidence if necessary.
3. Support investigates and sends findings, reasons and remedies. If more time is needed, it explains why and provides an estimate;
   mandatory legal deadlines remain applicable.
4. Reply using the same reference if unresolved or disputed; request review by Salman Alfariesh. BPSK, courts and competent authorities remain available.

Reports about a portfolio are sent through the **Report** link on it; the owner of a suspended portfolio can appeal to the email above
(see the [Terms](/legal/terms-of-service)).

Data requests follow applicable PDP deadlines, including 3 × 24 hours for actions subject to that limit.
Report suspected breaches without waiting for service hours. See the [Privacy policy](/legal/privacy-policy),
[Terms](/legal/terms-of-service) and [Refund policy](/legal/refund-policy).
