**Jobs** searches current openings for you. Listings come from job data providers that collect postings from company careers sites and job boards, so you don't need an account with them or any key or connection in Rezumi. Each listing links to its original posting, which is the one to apply through.

To open it, select **Applications** in the sidebar, then **Jobs** in the Applications menu (on a phone, the menu is the row of buttons at the top of the page).

## Search

The first time you open **Jobs**, the search starts from your [preferences](/guides/building-career-knowledge): the first role you want, the first place, and the country your searches target (**Where you're looking for work**; otherwise your account timezone's country). Nothing is searched until you select **Search**. If you have target roles, they show as buttons that search for that role straight away.

1. Type a role or keywords. Job titles work best: "data analyst" finds data analyst jobs, while a skill such as "python" finds only jobs with it in the title.
2. Choose how recent the postings should be (the last 30 days by default).
3. Select **Filters** for the country, city and everything else. The button names the place you search and how many other filters are set, for example **Filters · Indonesia (2)**.
4. Select **Search**, on the page or at the bottom of **Filters**.

Changing a filter doesn't search by itself. Set everything you want, then select **Search**. **Clear filters** in **Filters** goes back to a search anywhere with no filters.

| Filter                                  | Choices                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| --------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Country** and **City**                | A country, or **Anywhere**; and a city inside it, or the whole country when empty.                                                                                                                                     |
| **Posted**                              | The last day, week, two weeks or 30 days (the default).                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Work arrangement**                    | On-site, hybrid or remote (the default); **Remote in this country** with a country chosen, **Remote anywhere** with **Anywhere**; **Hybrid**; **On-site**.                                                             |
| **Level**                               | Any level, or entry, junior, mid, senior, lead or executive.                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Minimum yearly pay** and **Currency** | An amount and currency (it starts with the country's). **Include jobs that don't list pay** is on, because many postings don't list pay, especially outside the US; turn it off to see only jobs that list enough pay. |
| **Offers visa sponsorship**             | Only jobs whose listing says it sponsors a visa. Few listings say either way, so this narrows results a lot.                                                                                                           |

Results come 20 at a time; select **Load more** under them for the next ones. The line above the results says how many there are and when they were fetched. Rezumi keeps search results for a few hours and shares them between everyone who searches the same thing, so a search can show listings fetched earlier.

## Recent searches

Rezumi remembers your last 10 searches, on every device you sign in on. When you open **Jobs** again (from another page, or later the same day), it shows your last search right away if you searched it today, since opening it again is free. Otherwise **Jobs** starts with **Recent job searches**: each one shows its role, place and filters, and either **Free today** (you opened it today, so it doesn't count again) or **Uses 1 search**. Select one to run it again. Over results, **Recent searches** lists them too.

Select **×** beside a search to remove it, or **Clear** to remove them all. Removing a search doesn't change your searches each day.

## Searches each day

Free includes 3 searches a day, Plus 15 and Pro AI 40. Each search counts, and so does each **Load more**. Opening the same search again on the same day doesn't count again. The top right of the page shows how many are left. When they're used up, results you already opened stay available, and searches start again at midnight in your account timezone. See [Plans and payments](/guides/plans).

Sometimes Rezumi limits new searches for everyone for the rest of the day. The page then says so and shows results saved earlier where there are some.

## Read a listing

Results are a column of listings. On a wide screen the first one opens beside them; select another to read it there instead. The open listing takes the colour of its company's logo. On a smaller screen nothing opens until you select a listing, which then opens over the results (from the bottom on a phone). A listing in full shows the description, what the job asks for, skills, and the company's industry and size when the provider has them, with **Track**, **Tailor resume** and **View original**. Listings show the provider's text as it was published, so check the original posting before you apply.

Each listing shows what it states: how it's worked, the listed pay in its own currency, **Visa sponsorship**, the level, when it was posted and where it was found ("via LinkedIn", or "via the company's careers site").

## See how you fit

A green **Matches 4 of 6 skills** chip on a listing says how many of the main skills it names (up to 12) appear in your [Profile](/guides/updating-your-profile) and your switched-on facts. It's a word match, free and without AI: "SQL" in your Profile matches "SQL", but "analysed data" doesn't match "data analysis". The chip shows only when at least one skill matches. In the listing's **Skills**, the ones your Profile mentions have a check mark.

For a closer look, open the listing and select **Check fit** under **Your fit**. It runs the same check as **Fit** in an application's workspace: each requirement against your facts and stories, and the job against your preferences. See [Apply when your experience is different](/guides/checking-job-fit). On Rezumi AI it costs 4 credits, as in the workspace.

The result is kept. Opening the listing again shows it without running or charging again, and tracking the job copies it into the application's **Fit**, where you can add evidence and questions. If you change your Profile after the check, it says so and offers **Check again**, which runs (and costs) again.

## When a job closes

Every night Rezumi checks which listings their employers took down. Closed jobs stop showing in results and job alerts within about a day, and Rezumi removes its copy of them.

If you tracked the job, the application stays, with its saved copy of the posting. It shows **Posting closed** in its details and workspace. If you hadn't applied yet (it was still **Saved**), Home also tells you once.

## Track a job or tailor a resume

| Button            | What it does                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Track**         | Saves the job in [Applications](/guides/tracking-job-applications) as **Saved**, with the role, company, place, listed pay, the provider's copy of the description and the link to the original posting. The button becomes **Tracked**, which opens the application. |
| **Tailor resume** | Tracks the job if you haven't, then opens [tailoring a resume for it](/guides/tailoring-a-resume-for-a-job).                                                                                                                                                          |
| **View original** | Opens the original posting in a new tab.                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |

A job you already saved, from here, from a link or with the [browser extension](/guides/saving-jobs-from-your-browser), shows as **Tracked**; tracking it again doesn't make a second application.

To take a listing out of your results, open its **⋯** menu and select **Hide this job**. The notice offers **Undo**.

## Get new jobs for a search

To hear about new openings without searching again, select **Get job alerts** above the results. It saves the search as a [job alert](/guides/finding-roles-automatically): every day, every 3 days or every week, Rezumi looks for jobs that appeared since the last check and puts them in **Home** for you to review. The button reads **Job alert on** when an alert already repeats the search. Job alerts don't use your daily searches.

## Search from an AI client

With Rezumi's [MCP server](/guides/using-the-mcp-server#job-search) connected, you can ask Claude, ChatGPT or another client to search for you, for example "Find product designer jobs in Berlin posted this week and save the two best to my applications". It searches the same listings, uses the same searches a day, shows the link to each original posting, and saves a job as **Saved** in Applications when you ask.

## When nothing matches

- Choose a longer posting window, fewer filters or other words. Job titles work better than skills.
- Try **Anywhere** with **Remote anywhere** for remote work.
- For local job boards the providers don't cover, save jobs from your browser with the [browser extension](/guides/saving-jobs-from-your-browser), or paste a link in **Save job** on Applications.

If jobs can't be loaded, try again in a moment. Results you already opened stay available.
