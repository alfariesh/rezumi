“My interview is at 2pm and my mind is blank.” Open the application's workspace and select **Prepare**. A saved briefing gives you a focus, an opening, stories to bring and questions to ask.

If you have only a few minutes, go straight to [Read the short briefing](#read-the-short-briefing). Set up a schedule beforehand when you want the next briefing ready without opening the app.

## Add the interview details

Open the application's workspace. In **Next step**, choose **Edit** to schedule or edit the interview. Record the date, time, timezone, duration, who you will meet and what they asked you to prepare. See [Scheduling interviews](/guides/scheduling-interviews).

Check **What you sent** too. Preparation uses recorded sent resume and letter versions when available. Without a sent version, it can use the linked current document. A linked resume is not required to start preparation.

## Make a plan before the day

Each workspace section's AI button shows its cost on Rezumi AI: fit check 4, briefing, practice feedback or debrief 3, reply or answer draft 2.

1. Open **Prepare** and choose **Plan my preparation**.
2. Read the timed plan. Open a suggested story, draft a missing one, or rehearse a question.
3. Tick completed steps. Your progress stays with the application.
4. Review **Questions to ask**. Fit questions you saved for this job appear here too.

## Have the briefing ready in the morning

1. Check your timezone in **Settings → General**.
2. Open **Home → Schedules → Add**.
3. Choose **Prepare**, then the correct interview.
4. Choose **Morning of**, check the **AI connection**, and leave **Turn this schedule on** enabled.
5. Select **Save schedule**.

For a 14:00 interview, Morning of schedules the briefing at **08:00 in your account timezone**. For an interview before 10:00, it schedules two hours before the start instead. If the scheduled time has passed when you save, the work becomes due now.

You can choose **24 hours before** or **2 hours before** instead. The **Refresh the briefing 24 hours before** switch in Prepare is a shortcut for a 24-hour schedule, not an 08:00 schedule.

The browser can be closed. Delivery depends on the installation's scheduler and a working provider; a due time is not a guarantee of an exact arrival minute. When ready, the notification appears on **Home**. If it fails, check the notice and [schedule troubleshooting](/guides/career-workspace-reference#when-something-does-not-work).

In the mobile app, go to **App settings → AI & developer → AI data sharing** to review scheduled AI approvals. Open an interview preparation approval and choose **Withdraw approval** to pause that schedule across devices.

## Read the short briefing

From Home, select **Open briefing**, or use **Prepare → Read briefing**.

Read four things:

1. **Your focus:** What to make clear in this round.
2. **Your opening:** A starting point you can put into your own words.
3. **Two stories:** Real examples to have ready.
4. **Keep it honest:** What is not supported yet and how to handle it.

## Practice one answer

Open **Practice** from the plan, or select **Practice** in the workspace menu. Choose a question and select **Hear it** to have it read aloud.

Opening **Practice** from a story in your Profile keeps that story selected. Typed answers and corrected transcripts stay in this browser tab through navigation and refresh. Leaving the session stops recording and speech playback.

<Frame caption="Practice carries the selected story into its question. Interview notes stay separate from the people you will meet.">
  <img
    src="/images/guides/career/practise-story.png"
    alt="Practice, selected in the workspace menu, with the onboarding story named in its question, and a separate Interview notes heading under Next step"
  />

1. Choose **Speak**, then **Record your answer**. Allow the microphone if you want to use it.
2. Answer for up to two minutes, then stop.
3. Read the transcript and correct anything the transcription got wrong.
4. Select **Get feedback**.

Use **Voice settings** to choose who reads questions and who transcribes answers. Those can be different connections from the AI that reviews your answer. When no suitable speech connection is available, the app uses browser speech where supported. You can always choose **Type** instead.

Feedback explains **What works**, one thing to **Strengthen**, and an opening to try. Open a previous attempt to compare your answers. Practice questions are simulations; they are not a claim about what this employer will ask.

## Debrief while it is fresh

After the real interview, open **Debrief** (after the interview time, the workspace opens there):

1. Write the question they asked.
2. Record what you said as closely as you remember it.
3. Choose **Went well**, **Mixed** or **Didn't land** for how you felt it landed.
4. Select **Review my answer**.

The review separates supported claims from observations and suggests a practice priority for the next round. Recalled questions can feed later practice. Your impression of the interview is not proof of why an employer made a decision.
