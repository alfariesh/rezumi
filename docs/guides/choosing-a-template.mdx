[Browse the free resume template gallery](https://rezumi.app/resume-templates) to compare all 16 layouts and download sample PDFs before creating an account.

A template decides the layout of your resume: where the header sits, whether there is a sidebar, and how headings and entries look. You can switch templates at any time. Your content, fonts and colors stay the same unless you choose the template's own style.

## Open the template gallery

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your resume">From **Documents**, open the resume you want to change.</Step>
  <Step title="Switch to Design">
    Select **Design** at the top of the editor, or press <kbd>2</kbd> while you are not typing in a field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the Template group">
    **Template** is the first group in the Design panel. Use the row of links at the top of the panel to jump between
    groups.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each thumbnail shows the first page of **your** resume in that template, so you can judge it with your real content. While the thumbnails are being drawn, you briefly see the template's sample instead.

## Filter the gallery

The menu at the top picks a **field of work**: **Tech**, **Creative**, **Finance**, **Government & state-owned**, **Academic** or **Fresh graduate**. It shows the templates designed for that kind of job; **All fields** shows them all.

The chips below it narrow the list by layout:

| Filter          | Shows                                                                                                    |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **All**         | All 16 templates.                                                                                        |
| **One column**  | Templates that read top to bottom in a single column.                                                    |
| **Two columns** | Templates with a separate sidebar column.                                                                |
| **ATS-safe**    | Templates with one reading order and plain text headings, which applicant tracking systems read cleanly. |

The counter on the right (for example "4 of 16 shown") tells you how many templates match both.

## Preview and apply a template

<Steps>
  <Step title="Preview it on the page">
    Hover over a thumbnail, or move to it with <kbd>Tab</kbd>. The page on the right redraws in that template, and a
    label above it reads "Previewing *name* · click to apply". Nothing is saved yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply it">
    Click the thumbnail. The template is applied and a message confirms the change, with an **Undo** button if you
    change your mind.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or go back">
    Move the pointer away from the thumbnails, or press <kbd>Esc</kbd>, to return to your current template.
  </Step>
</Steps>

  On a phone or tablet, press and hold a thumbnail to preview it, then tap it to apply it. On phones, Design opens as a
  sheet over the lower half of the page and the templates scroll sideways in a strip.
</Tip>

Templates marked **Plus** come with the Plus and Pro AI plans. On the Free plan you can still preview them; applying one
explains the plan. Meridian, Sterling, Tunas, Wibawa and Kirana are free.

## Use the template's own style

Applying a template keeps your fonts and colors. Below the thumbnails, the **_name_ style** card shows the fonts and
accent color the current template was designed with. Select **Use _name_ style** to apply them, together with the
template's entry layout, how skill keywords print and the photo shape. Your content stays, and a message offers
**Undo**. When the resume already uses them, the button reads **Style in use**.

Two-column templates also show **Sidebar** with **Left** and **Right**, to move the sidebar to the other side. See
[Arranging the layout](/guides/arranging-the-layout).

## Tips for choosing

- **Applying through job portals?** Start with the **ATS-safe** filter. Two-column layouts can be read out of order by applicant tracking systems, and Check mode warns you when sidebar sections might be affected. See [Checking your resume](/guides/checking-your-resume).
- **Does the employer want a photo?** Local employers and the civil service often ask for one (Wibawa and Tenun have a place for it); multinational companies and ATS-heavy processes usually prefer none. Every template stays balanced without a photo.
- **Lots of experience?** Compare how full each thumbnail's first page looks. After you apply a template, the page count in the zoom bar at the bottom of the page tells you the total.
- **Judge with real content.** Because the gallery draws your own resume, fill in your details first and then pick.
- **Icons and logos.** Every template can show icons beside contact details, section titles and entries, and Plus and Pro AI can add tool logos to skills. See [Icons and logos](/guides/icons-and-logos).

## Change how entries look

The **Entries** group, below **Template**, arranges the header of each entry: the lines with the company or school,
the position, the location and the dates. These settings work with every template and stay when you switch templates.

### Header layout

| Layout          | What it does                                                                                                                                             |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Stacked**     | Two rows: the company and location, then the position and dates. This is the default.                                                                    |
| **One line**    | Puts the company, the position and the dates on one line, wrapping onto the next when they don't fit. Education adds a line with the grade and location. |
| **Compact**     | Puts the company and the dates on the first row, then the position and location together on one line.                                                    |
| **Date column** | Sets the dates in a narrow column at the start, with the company, position and location beside them.                                                     |
| **Eyebrow**     | Puts the position and location in a small line in the accent color above a larger company name.                                                          |

**One line** and **Date column** need the full width of the main column. In the sidebar, or in a section split into
more than one column, entries use **Stacked** instead. **Date column** also moves the dates of Projects, Awards,
Certifications and Publications into the column.

### Row order

**Row order** shows the two header rows, for example "Company · Location" above "Position · Date". Select the swap
button (⇅) to put the position first in every layout, and select it again to put the company back. Education
swaps the school and the field of study the same way. Volunteer entries always start with the organization, and an entry
with nothing to swap keeps the company or school first.

In the web editor, you can also swap the rows from the page: select an Experience or Education entry, then select
**Swap rows** on its **Editing** tag.

### Accent

**Accent** adds the accent color to each entry header: **None** (the default), **Rule** (a line under it), **Bar** (a bar
at its start) or **Band** (a light tint behind it). In a colored sidebar, the accent uses the sidebar's text color
instead.

The **Entries** group works the same way on a phone and in the Android and iPhone apps, where it is a tab in the design
sheet. DOCX and ODT downloads follow **Row order** but keep their plain layout, without header layouts or accents. Custom
styles still apply on top of these settings; see [Applying custom styles](/applying-custom-styles).

## Reset the design settings

If you have changed a lot of design settings and want a clean slate, open **Advanced** at the bottom of the Design panel and select **Reset design defaults**. This resets fonts, text size, line height, colors, level style, the **Entries** settings, sidebar width, margins, spacing and the icon and underline switches. Your template, paper size, language, date format, section placement and custom CSS stay as they are. A message offers **Undo**.

The reset doesn't depend on the template: every template goes back to the same standard settings (IBM Plex Serif at 10 pt, a red accent, circle levels, stacked entry headers and hidden section icons). Pick a font pairing and accent color again afterwards if you want a different starting point.

<Note>
  If Design's controls are greyed out, the resume is locked. Open the document name menu at the top left and select
  **Unlock editing**.
</Note>
