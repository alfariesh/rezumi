**Applications** is where you keep track of the jobs you're going for. Each application holds the role, the company, the saved job posting, the resume and letter you sent, your contacts, notes and a dated history. It also works out what each application needs next, such as an interview coming up or a follow-up that's due.

For focused work on one job, open its details and select **Open workspace**. [Career and Application Workspaces](/guides/career-overview) explains Fit, Apply, interview preparation, practice, replies and saved results. Shared experience lives in your **Profile**; search schedules are on **Home**.

To open it, select **Applications** in the sidebar (on a phone, open the menu at the top left first). Its menu on the left (a row of chips on a phone) has three pages: **All**, your applications; **Insights**, charts about how they're going; and [**Offers**](/guides/comparing-job-offers), saved offers side by side.

## How stages work

Every application sits in one stage. The first five stages form a pipeline you move through in order:

| Stage         | Use it when                                                              |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Saved**     | You found the job but haven't applied yet.                               |
| **Applied**   | You sent your application.                                               |
| **Screening** | A recruiter or hiring manager got back to you, or you have a first call. |
| **Interview** | You're in the interview process.                                         |
| **Offer**     | You received an offer.                                                   |
| **Closed**    | The application ended, whatever the outcome.                             |

You can close an application from any stage. Closing asks for a reason (**Not selected**, **I withdrew**, **Accepted another offer** or **No response**), which Insights uses later. Closed applications stay in the List's **Closed** group and the Board's **Closed** column. See [Managing an application](/guides/managing-an-application#closing-an-application).

<Note>
  When an application with a linked resume or letter reaches **Applied** or a later stage, Rezumi saves a copy of each
  as a "Sent to *company*" version. You can always open exactly what you sent, even after you keep editing the document.
</Note>

## Choosing a layout

On **All**, the switch to the right of the search, three icons, lays your applications out as a **List**, a **Board** or a **Calendar**. Rezumi remembers your choice on this device, and the page address keeps it, so a bookmark opens the same layout.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="List">
    The default layout. Applications are grouped by stage in the order that usually needs you first: **Interview**, **Offer**, **Screening**, **Applied**, **Saved**, then **Closed**.

    - Select a group heading to collapse or expand it.
    - Select the **Role**, **Next step** or **Updated** column heading to sort within each group. Select it again to reverse the order.
    - The **Next step** column shows the next interview, the follow-up you set, or how long you've been waiting. It turns amber when something is overdue or you've had no reply for 10 days or more.
    - The **Sent** column shows an icon for a linked resume and a linked letter. It says **None** in amber when an application was sent without a linked document.
    - Select a row to open its details.

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Board">
    One column per stage, with a card for each application. Drag a card to another column to move it to that stage. Each card also has a **⋯** menu with **Move to…**, **Close…** and **Delete…**, which is handy if you prefer the keyboard. The last column is **Closed**. Dragging a card there asks why it ended before closing it, so Insights can use the outcome.

Phones show the list only, without the switch, and list rows show the company and next step on a second line.

**Insights**, in the menu, is a page of its own: how far your applications get, how often and how quickly people reply, and where they come from. See [Viewing application insights](/guides/viewing-application-insights).

## Finding applications

Type in the search field (**Search role, company or contact**) to filter your applications as you type. Search looks at the role, company, location, contact names and tags. If nothing matches, select **Clear search**.

Closed applications appear in the list, board and calendar, and Insights counts every application.

## Acting on several applications at once

In the list on a computer or tablet, select the checkboxes next to the applications you want. A bar appears at the bottom of the page with:

- **Move to…** to move them all to one stage
- **Add tag** to add the same tag to each
- **Close…** to close them all with one reason
- **Delete…** to delete them permanently (you're asked to confirm)
- **×** (**Clear selection**) to deselect

Selection follows the visible rows. Collapsing a stage or changing the search removes hidden selections before a bulk action.

## Follow-up reminders

When an application has been at **Applied** for 10 days or more without a reply, a reminder appears above the list for the one that has waited longest, for example "Harborlight Labs: no reply for 17 days." Select **Open** to go to that application, or **×** to dismiss the reminder. Dismissing is remembered in this browser, so that application's reminder doesn't come back here.

## Opening Applications from anywhere

Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> on Windows and Linux) and choose **Applications** to go to the page or to search your applications by company or role. Run **New application** to open the add dialog. See [Using the command bar](/guides/using-the-command-bar).
