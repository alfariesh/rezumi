Your portfolio is a website at `rezumi.app/your-username`. Its home page shows your work, results, services and contact details, and each work has a page of its own. Career details come from your [Profile](/guides/using-your-profile) or one of your resumes, so the page stays up to date when they change.

## Before you start

- Check your username in **Account**. It's the address of your portfolio.
- Verify your email address. You can build a portfolio without it, but you need it to publish.
- Fill in your [Profile](/guides/using-your-profile), or have a resume ready, for a head start. You can change where the content comes from later in **Publish**.

## The editor

Three modes run down the rail at the left edge of the window: **Content**, **Design** and **Publish**; the panel beside it shows the one you pick. The preview on the right shows the page as visitors will see it; **Computer** and **Phone** in the middle of the editor bar switch its width. On a phone the preview fills the screen, and **Content**, **Design** and **Publish** float at the bottom; each opens its panel in a sheet over the page.

Changes save on their own, and a cloud icon at the top shows the state. **Undo** and **Redo** sit in the preview's header, beside **Computer** and **Phone**, and <kbd>⌘Z</kbd> and <kbd>⌘⇧Z</kbd> work outside text fields. If you edit the same portfolio in another tab or on another device, the editor tells you and asks you to reload, so neither copy overwrites the other.

## Write your content

**Intro** is always first. Your name, headline and location come from your Profile or source resume. Type in the fields to use different ones on the portfolio only, or leave them empty to follow the source. Add a short greeting, choose whether your photo shows, and add up to three buttons: view your resume, download its PDF, send a message, WhatsApp, email, **Save contact**, a profile or another link. Turn off **Show my location** to keep your location off the page, including the one from your Profile or resume.

Turn on **Open to work** to say you're looking, with your own wording if you like. Under **Roles you're looking for**, add up to five roles, such as Product Designer, and under **Kinds of work**, tick full-time, part-time, contract, freelance or internship. The page shows them as one line, such as "Looking for: Product Designer — Full-time". Anyone who opens your page sees this, your current employer too.

Below it are your sections. Drag a section by its handle, or use its arrows, to change the order. The eye hides a section, and the pencil renames it (leave the name empty to use the default).

| Section          | What you can add                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **About me**     | Rich text: bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, headings, lists, quotes and links                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Services**     | A name, description, optional price ("From Rp1.5m"), icon, and an order button (WhatsApp with a message naming the service, Contact, or a link)                                                                                              |
| **Testimonials** | Someone's words, once you confirm they're genuine and that the person agreed to show them, with their photo if you like                                                                                                                      |
| **Clients**      | Names, with a logo you upload (PNG or WebP with a clear background; uploading one confirms you may show it) or a brand logo when one is known. **Add from Experience** adds the companies in your Experience                                 |
| Career sections  | Experience, Education, Skills and others, from your Profile or source resume. Switch entries off to hide them on the portfolio only; your Profile and resumes don't change. With a source resume, **Edit on resume** opens the resume editor |
| **Contact**      | An invitation line, which details show (email and website on, phone off by default), a WhatsApp button, a **Save contact** button, your profiles, the contact form and the location card                                                     |

**Add a section** adds one of your own (up to ten), with a title, text, an optional picture and an optional button.

An **Email** or **WhatsApp** button shows that address to visitors even if **Contact** hides it.

**Save contact** gives visitors a contact card to save to their phone, with your name, headline and the contact details **Contact** shows. Turn on **Show a Save contact button** under **Contact**, or add it as one of the intro's buttons.

### The location card

In **Contact**, find your city (the list starts from the location in your Profile or source resume), then choose how precise to be: city, province or state, or country. You can also say how you work, whether you're open to relocating, and show your local time.

**Show a map on my page** is off by default. Turned on, it shows a map you can zoom, and at city precision you can drag the pin around your city. It's rounded to about a kilometre, but don't put it on your home. Your page shows a picture of the map in light and dark mode, so visitors' browsers never contact a map service.

## Add your work

Each work has a **card** in the list and a **page** of its own that opens when a visitor selects the card. **Add work** offers four ways in:

- **Empty**: fill in the card, then build the page.
- **From my resume**: tick projects on your source resume, or in your Profile when it's the source. Their title, summary, dates and link come along.
- **Upload pictures**: choose, drop or paste many pictures at once, then make one work with all of them (up to 12) or one work per picture, which suits photographers and illustrators.
- **Paste a link**: paste a link to Behance, Dribbble, GitHub, Figma, Medium, YouTube, Vimeo or any public page. The title, summary and a picture fill in, and you can change them. Use the picture as the cover only if you may show it, because it's copied into your portfolio.

Open a work to edit it. The preview shows its page while you do, and **All work** in the preview takes you back to the home page.

The **card** has a cover (select the part to keep in view when the card crops it), title, a short summary, your role, the year, up to 12 tags, the **Metric on the card**, a main link, **Featured** and **Hide from the page**.

### A work's own page

Every work you show has its own address, such as `rezumi.app/your-username/work/qr-checkout`, so you can send someone straight to one project. The page has the work's title, summary, role and year, its blocks, and links to the previous and next work. **All work** goes back to your home page, and so does the browser's back button.

The address is made from the work's title and follows it when you rename the work. To choose your own, type it in **Page address**: lowercase letters, numbers and dashes. **Open page** opens the work's page on your published portfolio. Changing the address keeps the old one working: it forwards to the new one, so links you've already shared still lead there.

A hidden work, a work without a title, or every work while the **Work** section is hidden has no page.

### Blocks

A work's page is built from blocks. Use **Add block** between blocks, or type `/` in a text block.

| Block        | What it does                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Text**     | Paragraphs, headings, lists, quotes and links                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Pictures** | 1 to 12 pictures with a description and optional caption, shown full width, as a grid, or to swipe sideways. Visitors can open them full screen                                                   |
| **Metrics**  | 1 to 4 results as numbers, such as "+35% checkout conversion". Use numbers you can explain in an interview, and say where they come from                                                          |
| **Video**    | A YouTube or Vimeo link. Visitors see the thumbnail and a play button; the player, and its tracking, only load when they press play                                                               |
| **PDF**      | A PDF up to 10 MB, without a password. Its first page becomes the picture, and **Open PDF** opens it in a new tab. Details saved inside the PDF, such as the author's name, are published with it |
| **Link**     | A card with the title, description, site name and the site's logo when known. The page's picture is only a suggestion: **Use this picture** copies it into your portfolio                         |
| **Embed**    | A Figma, Loom, Spotify, SoundCloud or Google Slides link that plays or opens on the page itself, after a visitor presses it                                                                       |

Drag a block by its handle or use its arrows to reorder. Removing a block can be undone. An empty work can **Start from a case study outline**: headings for the problem, your role, the process and the result, and a Metrics block.

### Embeds

An **Embed** block shows a Figma file or prototype, a Loom video, a Spotify track, album, playlist or episode, a SoundCloud track or set, or a Google Slides presentation on your work's page. Paste its link in **Add an embed**. If you paste one of these links into a Link block, Rezumi offers to **Embed it instead**.

Visitors first see a button with the service's name, or a picture you choose. Nothing loads from the service, and nothing tracks them, until they press **Show … here**; the page says which service it loads content from. The content has to be visible to anyone with the link: share a Figma file that way, and share a Google Slides presentation with **Anyone with the link** first, or it won't show.

### Metrics

A metric has a **value** (a number, or short text such as "Top 3"), how it's written (number, percent, rupiah, dollar, short number like 1.2k, times like 3×, a duration, or a rating out of 5), what goes before or after it ("+", "/month"), and a **label**.

You can also add a change (up, down or the same, and whether it's good news, since a fall can be good), the value before, a small chart of 3 to 12 values, progress from 0 to 100, its context or source, and an icon. Numbers are written the way the page's language writes them. The **Metrics** layout in **Design** chooses KPI cards, big numbers, or before → after.

### Pictures

Pictures are made smaller in your browser before they're uploaded: at most 2,000 pixels on the longest side (logos 600), as WebP. This also removes hidden data such as where a photo was taken. JPG, PNG, WebP and still GIF pictures work; SVG files and animated GIFs don't.

Every picture needs a short description for people who can't see it. Pictures you remove or replace are deleted a day later, so **Undo** can still bring them back.

## Design your page

In **Design**, choose:

- The theme, in the same gallery as when you created the portfolio.
- Light, dark, or **Device**, which follows each visitor's own setting.
- An accent colour: the same as your resume, one of eight, or your own. A colour that's hard to read as text is adjusted for text automatically.
- A font pair, and the layout of each section the theme supports.

Under **Intro**, choose the intro's background (plain, a soft gradient, a subtle pattern, or your own picture under a darker layer) and an effect for your name or headline. Each theme offers the effects that suit it.

| Effect              | What it does                                                                    |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Gentle fade-in**  | Words appear one by one                                                         |
| **Shimmer**         | A light in your accent colour sweeps across                                     |
| **Living gradient** | Your accent colours drift, then rest                                            |
| **Typewriter**      | Your headline types itself, then up to two more roles, and ends on the headline |
| **Tech scramble**   | Random letters settle into your name                                            |
| **Vector outline**  | Letters turn into outlines under the mouse. Phones get the gentle fade-in       |

Effects stop by themselves within five seconds, never hide your name from search engines or screen readers, and stay still for visitors who ask their device for less motion. **Replay intro** above the preview plays the effect again.

## Publish

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Publish">
    In the **Publish** tab, turn on **Published**. It shows your address and asks whether search engines may list your
    portfolio (on by default).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check where your resume moves">
    If your short link opened your resume until now, it says where the resume is now:
    `rezumi.app/your-username/your-resume`. Your portfolio links to it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share it">Copy the address, select **Open page**, or open your **Business card**.</Step>
</Steps>

Also in **Publish**:

- Turning **Published** off takes the page down. Your short link opens your primary resume again.
- Set the title and description that search results show. New accounts are kept out of search engines for their first seven days, even with listing on.
- **Page language** changes the page's own words (section titles, button labels), not your text.
- **Source** → **Content from** chooses where your career sections come from: **Your Profile** or one of your resumes. With your Profile, your page shows your Profile's details and career as they are now, in the page's default language. If a source resume is moved to Trash, your portfolio keeps its own content and asks you to choose another source.

### Your business card

**Business card** in **Publish** shows a card with your name, headline, your portfolio's address and a QR code that opens it. From there:

- **Download QR (PNG)** or **Download QR (SVG)** saves the code for slides, posters or your own card design.
- **Print cards** makes an A4 sheet of 10 cards (85 × 55 mm) with lines to cut along.
- **Show full screen** shows the code large on your screen, for someone at a booth or across the table to scan.

The QR code adds `?utm_source=qr` to your address, so visits from it show as **QR code** in your statistics.

### Protect it with a password

Turn on **Protect with a password** in **Publish**, type a password of at least 6 characters, and select **Set password**. Give it only to the people who should see your portfolio.

- Visitors see a page that says the portfolio is private, without your name, work or details, and a **Password** field. Once they enter it, your portfolio and every work's page open in that browser for 12 hours.
- Pictures and PDFs on your portfolio open only for visitors who entered the password.
- Search engines don't list your portfolio while it has a password, and link previews show a general picture instead of yours. **Show in search engines** is off until you remove the password.
- **Change password** ends every visitor's access: everyone who opened it with the old password, and every file link they had, needs the new one.
- After several wrong tries, a visitor waits 10 minutes. After about 100 wrong passwords in an hour, from anyone, your portfolio refuses every try for an hour, the right one too.
- **Remove password** makes your portfolio public again.

## Your short link and your resume

While your portfolio is published, `rezumi.app/your-username` and your own domain open the portfolio. Your resume stays at its own address, and its **Share** panel says so. Unpublish the portfolio and the short link opens your primary resume again.

## Connect your own domain

Plus and Pro AI accounts can open their portfolio at a domain they own, such as `www.yourname.com`. Go to **Publish** and find **Your domain**, or use **Your domain** on the Portfolio page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your domain">
    A name with `www` or another subdomain works with every domain company. A domain without `www` only works if your
    DNS provider allows a CNAME there. If it doesn't, use `www` and forward the plain domain to it where you manage the
    domain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the two records">
    Add the CNAME and TXT records shown where you manage your domain. If your DNS is on Cloudflare, set the CNAME to
    **DNS only**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check DNS">
    Select **Check DNS**. The steps tick off as your domain is set up, and HTTPS usually takes a few minutes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Keep both records in place. Each account has one domain. When a paid plan ends, your domain keeps working for 7 days, then goes to your rezumi.app address. Renew and it comes back by itself, with no DNS change.

## See visitors and messages

The **Portfolio** page in your dashboard shows your numbers once you've published. Only you can see them.

- **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days** or **Last 90 days** chooses the period. The address keeps your choice, so a reload or a bookmark opens the same period. Days are counted in your timezone; **Change timezone** sets it.
- **Visitors**: visits per day, clicks on your buttons (including videos played, embeds opened and contacts saved), messages, and where visits came from: LinkedIn, Google, **QR code** (your business card), WhatsApp, **Direct** or **Other sites**. A visitor counts once an hour. Your own visits, search engines and previews don't count, and no tracking cookie is used.
- **Per work**: how often each work's page was opened, and its share of all openings. **Details** on a work shows how often its page was opened per day, and with Plus its visitors' countries, devices and the sites that linked to it.
- **Download CSV** saves **Daily numbers** (visits, messages, clicks and sources per day) or **Works** (how often each work was opened), and with Plus the **Breakdown**: countries, devices, linking sites and works by day. Spreadsheet apps open it directly.
- **With Plus**: visitors' countries on a map, their devices, the sites that link to you, and how often your works were opened each day. These figures are estimates.

To receive messages, turn on **Show a contact form** under **Contact** in the editor. Visitors pass a quick anti-spam check. Each message arrives by email (reply to that email to answer the sender) and in **Messages** on the Portfolio page, where you can mark it as read or delete it. Messages are deleted automatically after 90 days. If several arrive close together, one email sums them up.

The form needs JavaScript in the visitor's browser; without it, visitors see your other contact options. On your own domain, the form's button opens your portfolio on rezumi.app to send the message.

## Write with the assistant

Select the **AI** icon at the top right of the editor, or press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>J</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>J</kbd>), to open the assistant beside the page. Tell it what you want in your own words, for example:

- "Add my Lumen redesign as a work" (attach screenshots, and it uses them as the cover and gallery, with alternative text)
- "Pick a theme and colours that suit a product designer"
- "Rewrite my intro so it says who I help"
- "Hide my oldest internship and move Services above Experience"
- "Translate the whole page into English"

Unlike the resume assistant, it **changes your portfolio straight away**: the preview updates as it works and the change saves like any edit you make. Each reply ends with a card listing what changed. Select a line to open that work or section, and **Undo** on the card takes back everything that reply changed (then **Redo** puts it back). The editor's own **Undo** works too.

What it does and doesn't do:

- It only uses facts from your portfolio, your Profile or source resume, the files you attach and what you tell it. When something's missing, such as a project's result, it asks instead of making it up.
- Career sections (Experience, Education, Skills and the rest) come from your Profile or resume: it can hide items, rename and reorder them, but rewrites happen there.
- It adds a testimonial only with words you give it, after you confirm the person agreed to be shown.
- It can turn contact details off but not on, and it never publishes, sets a password or changes your address or domain: those stay with you in **Content** and **Publish**.
- Themes and effects that need Plus are suggested, not applied, on a Free plan.

Each reply costs 3 credits on Rezumi AI, like the resume assistant; on your own AI connection it costs nothing. See [Using the assistant](/guides/using-the-assistant) for conversations, attachments and models.

## Write with AI

- **Write a draft with AI** at the top of **Content** writes your greeting, **About me** and the works you pick from your Profile or source resume, for 3 credits. It replaces what's in those places, and **Undo** takes it back.
- The small **AI** button on long fields makes the text shorter, more professional, or translates it to the page's language, for 1 credit. Testimonials don't have it: they're someone else's words.
- **Find numbers** beside a metric list reads your jobs and projects and suggests metrics, each with the sentence it comes from, for 1 credit. Only numbers written in your Profile or source resume are suggested, and you pick which to add.

Failed or stopped requests give their credits back. On your own AI connection, nothing costs credits. See [Using AI](/guides/using-ai).

## Reports and takedowns

Every published portfolio has a **Report** link in its footer. A visitor picks a reason, describes the problem, and can leave an email if they'd like a reply. You never see who reported your portfolio.

If the Rezumi team takes your portfolio down, visitors stop seeing it, your short link opens your primary resume if you have one, and you can't publish or delete it. Nothing is deleted. The **Publish** tab shows the reason. To appeal, email hi@rezumi.app.

## Delete your portfolio

On the **Portfolio** page, **Delete portfolio** removes its text, work, design and settings for good. Your resumes and Profile stay as they are. A published page goes away with it, and your short link opens your primary resume again.
