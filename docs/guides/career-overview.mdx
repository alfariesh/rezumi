You have an interview this afternoon, another application form to finish, and a recruiter waiting for a reply. Career helps you carry the same experience through all three, without starting over each time.

Start with facts you know are true and stories about work you have done. Use them across your search, then keep each job's posting, answers, interview preparation and messages in its own workspace.

## Two places, two purposes

**Coach**, in the bar at the top of every page, opens a conversation about your search across jobs in a panel beside the page. **Past conversations** in the panel lists your earlier ones, and **Open full screen** gives the coach the whole page.

Two more places in the sidebar are for your whole search:

- **Home**, the first page after you sign in, opens on previews of the documents you edited last, then brings together reminders, new roles, your next interview and your schedules. Notifications you have read are removed after 90 days; unread ones stay until you read them.
- **Profile** holds your details and career history, plus the facts, stories and preferences Career works from. See [Keep your details in one Profile](/guides/using-your-profile).

**Applications** is your job tracker. **Offers**, the last item in its menu, compares saved offers side by side. Open an application and select **Open workspace** for tools dedicated to that job, listed in a menu on the left: **Fit**, **Apply**, **Prepare**, **Practice**, **Debrief**, **Messages** and **Saved**. **Ask the coach** opens a conversation beside the section you are using, with that application's context.

In the native iPhone app, application details name the relevant task instead: **Prepare application**, **Prepare for interview**, or **Draft a follow-up**, depending on the next step. See [native iPhone controls](/guides/editing-on-mobile#native-iphone-app).

## Keep your experience honest

A **fact** is something you state or confirm, such as what you did, when you did it, or a result you measured. A **story** puts that experience into five parts: Situation, Task, Action, Result and Reflection. Link stories to the facts that support them.

These are your records, not independent checks by an employer or a background-check service. You decide whether each fact is accurate. Leave a story's Result blank when you cannot support it. If you correct a linked fact later, the story is flagged for review.

**Fit** shows the evidence behind each requirement. **No evidence yet** means your facts do not show it; it does not mean you cannot do the job. A related experience can be useful without becoming a claim that you have done something you have not.

AI drafts are built from the experience you supply. Read them before using them, especially names, dates, your personal contribution and results. [Save your facts and stories](/guides/building-career-knowledge), or [check a role when your experience is different](/guides/checking-job-fit).

## Tell it once, use it again

The same shared fact or story can help with an application answer, an interview opening, a practice answer or a recruiter reply. You still review each draft for its audience.

Job-specific notes stay with that application. A fact marked private to one application stays in that scope until you choose **Use for all applications**. Shared facts let you reuse experience without treating every conversation with an employer as a fact about every job.

## Let scheduled work help

Enable schedules when you want job alerts, interview briefings or follow-up reminders to run while your browser is closed. Results wait on **Home** for you to review. Finding a role does not apply for it; reading a message does not send a reply.

Briefings need an AI connection; job alerts and reminders don't. A job alert brings only openings that appeared since its last run, and a run may find few or no new roles. [Set up a job alert](/guides/finding-roles-automatically) or [prepare for interview day](/guides/preparing-for-interview-day).

## Choose your AI and control your data

Career tracking and insights are available on every Rezumi plan. You can use your own AI connection, including OpenAI, DeepSeek, Google Gemini or another supported provider, and choose a default. Provider usage can cost money; it is billed through the connection you use. [Connect an AI provider](/guides/using-ai).

Each AI action has a line explaining what it sends and which connection receives it. In the coach, context controls let you choose what accompanies the next message. Editing facts, stories and preferences and the offer comparison itself do not send an AI request. Previously sent information remains subject to your provider's policies.

You can correct facts, switch them off for coaching, forget them, and export your account data. See [controls and data reference](/guides/career-workspace-reference#your-data) for the different effects of these actions.
