---
title: "Icons and logos"
description: "Show icons beside your contact details, section titles and entries, pick their style, choose an icon for any line, and add tool logos to your skills with Plus or Pro AI."
---

Every template can draw small icons: before your email and phone, before section titles such as **Education**, and
beside skills, interests and profiles. The icons come in three styles, and you can pick one for any line yourself. With
Plus or Pro AI, skills can also show the logos of the tools they name, such as React, Python or Figma.

Icons are drawings, not text. Applicant tracking systems read your resume the same way with or without them, and a
logo never replaces the word it stands for.

## Turn icons on and off

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Icons">
    In the editor, select **Design** (or press <kbd>2</kbd>), then select **Icons** in the row of links at the top of
    the panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Show or hide all icons">
    **Show icons** turns every icon on or off at once. Turn it off for a text-only resume.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose where they show">With icons on, each switch below controls one place on the page.</Step>
</Steps>

| Switch                             | Shows icons                                                                    |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Contact line**                   | Before your email, phone, location, website and custom fields.                 |
| **Section titles**                 | Before titles such as Education, Skills and Certifications.                    |
| **Skills, interests and profiles** | The icons you pick for entries, and network logos such as LinkedIn and GitHub. |
| **Tool logos in skills**           | Logos beside the tools a skill names. Plus and Pro AI; see below.              |

The switches belong to the resume, so they stay when you change templates.

## Pick an icon style

**Icon style** draws every icon on the page in one of three styles: **Standard** (outlined), **Rounded** (softer
corners) or **Sharp** (square ends). The style follows the icons in the editor too, so what you pick there matches the
page.

## Choose an icon for a line

You can replace the icon of any contact field, entry or section:

- **Custom contact fields**: in **Basics**, select the icon button at the start of the field.
- **Skills, interests and profiles**: open the entry and select its icon button. A profile without an icon of its own
  uses its network's logo.
- **Sections**: open the section's menu and select **Icon…**. The icon shows when **Section titles** is on.

The picker groups the icons by category, such as **Business**, **Education** and **Programming**, with the most used
ones under **Suggested**. Type in **Search for an icon** to find one by name or meaning; searching in Indonesian works
too ("surel" finds the email icon). Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to pick the best match. **No icon** removes the icon from
that line.

## Add tool logos to your skills

<Note>Tool logos are part of the **Plus** and **Pro AI** plans.</Note>

With **Tool logos in skills** on, Rezumi draws a logo beside each tool it recognises in your skills. Write the tool's
usual name, such as **TypeScript**, **PostgreSQL**, **React**, **Figma**, **Photoshop**, **Excel** or **Notion**; the
word always prints after the logo, so a parser still reads it. Names Rezumi doesn't know print as plain text.

A skill whose name is a tool gets the logo before its name. Keywords get logos when they print as chips: in **Write**,
open the Skills section's menu, select **Keyword layout**, then **Chips**. Vector's own style (**Use Vector style** in
Design → Template) prints keywords as chips.

On the Free plan the switch shows **Plus** and explains the plans. If a resume already shows logos, for example one you
imported, it keeps them until you turn the switch off.
