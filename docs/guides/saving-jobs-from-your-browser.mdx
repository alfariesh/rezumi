---
title: "Saving jobs from your browser"
description: "Save a job posting to your applications from LinkedIn, Indeed, JobStreet, Glints or any other site with the Rezumi browser extension for Chrome and Edge."
---

The Rezumi browser extension saves a job posting to your [applications](/guides/tracking-job-applications) while you are looking at it, so you don't copy links and text by hand. It also marks postings you already saved, shows how well a resume fits, and reminds you to follow up. It works in Chrome and Microsoft Edge on a computer, and it is free on every plan.

The extension only helps you save and prepare. Your jobs, notes and reminders live in your Rezumi account, so everything you save also appears in **Applications** on the web.

<Note>
  The extension reads a job page only when you ask: when you open its panel on the page, press **Save to Rezumi**, use
  the right-click menu or use the keyboard shortcut. It never presses a button on a job site for you, and it sends
  nothing about the pages you browse.
</Note>

## Install and connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the extension">
    Open the Chrome Web Store (Chrome) or Edge Add-ons (Edge), search for **Rezumi**, and add it. Pin it to the toolbar
    so the icon is always one click away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect to Rezumi">
    Select the Rezumi icon to open the side panel, then **Connect to Rezumi**. A Rezumi window asks you to sign in and
    allow access. You don't copy any key. If you don't have an account yet, select **Sign up free**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a job posting">
    Go to a posting. On LinkedIn, Indeed, JobStreet and Glints a **Save to Rezumi** button appears next to the site's
    own buttons.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Save a job

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the panel">
    Select the Rezumi icon, select the **Save to Rezumi** button on the page, or press <kbd>Alt</kbd> <kbd>Shift</kbd>{" "}
    <kbd>S</kbd>. You can change the shortcut in your browser's extension shortcut settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check what was read">
    The panel shows the **Role**, **Company**, **Location**, **Salary** and **Employment type** it found, and the
    description under **Description**. Edit anything that is wrong or missing. Only **Role** and **Company** are
    required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a status, tags and a reminder">
    **Saved** is the default. Choose **Already applied** only if you did, and confirm the date. You can also link a
    resume, add tags, and set a follow-up date with a note.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select **Save to Rezumi**. The job is added to your applications, and the button on the page turns into a **Saved**
    mark with its status.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you are not sure the page was read well, expand **Description** and compare it with the posting. If the text is clipped or incomplete, the panel says so.

### Sites other than the four

On any other site the extension does nothing until you click the toolbar icon, press <kbd>Alt</kbd> <kbd>Shift</kbd>{" "}

<kbd>S</kbd>, or select text and choose **Save selected text to Rezumi** from the right-click menu. It reads the
posting's structured job data when the page has it (the data Google uses for job listings), then the page text. If no
posting is found, select the description on the page and use the right-click menu, or paste it into the panel.

### Read with AI

**Read with AI** is off by default. Without it, the fields come from the page. Turn it on to have AI fill in more of them; it uses 1 credit on Rezumi AI, or your own AI connection if you set one up in [Using AI](/guides/using-ai). The panel shows the cost and your balance before you turn it on.

## Saved marks

When you open a posting you already saved, the button shows **Saved** with its status, and the toolbar icon gets a green check. The same posting is not saved twice by accident: the panel opens your existing application instead, with a small **Save as a new application** link if you really want another one.

<Frame caption="After saving, the button becomes a Saved mark with the job's status.">
  <img
    src="/images/guides/saving-jobs-from-your-browser/saved-chip.webp"

This check happens inside your browser. The extension downloads the list of postings you saved and compares it on your computer, so the addresses of the pages you visit are not sent to Rezumi.

If the same role at the same company is already saved from another site, the panel warns you and lets you save anyway.

## See how well your resume fits

Choose a resume under **Match** to see the share of the posting's terms your resume already contains, with the missing ones listed first. It is a free keyword comparison, not an AI judgement, and it works best with English postings and resumes. Use it as a hint for what to add or reword; it doesn't predict whether you'll be hired.

## After you save

The panel for a saved job lets you change the status, add notes and set a reminder without leaving the posting.

<Frame caption="A saved job: change its status, then open the preparation tools in Rezumi.">
  <img

    style={{ maxWidth: "320px" }}

- **Tailor resume** opens Rezumi with the copy-for-a-job dialog for this application. See [Tailoring a resume for a job](/guides/tailoring-a-resume-for-a-job).
- **Write cover letter** creates a cover letter linked to the job and opens it in the editor.
- **Open in Rezumi** opens the application. See [Managing an application](/guides/managing-an-application).

### Follow-up reminders

Set **Follow up on** to a date, with an optional note. When the posting states a closing date, the date starts two days before it; change or clear it. Reminders due today or earlier appear on the panel's home screen under **Due**, where **Done** clears them, and the toolbar icon shows their number.

<Frame caption="The home screen lists reminders that are due and your latest saved jobs.">

To get a desktop notification as well, open the panel's **Settings** and turn on **Desktop reminders**. Your browser asks for permission once. You get at most one notification per application per day, at 09:00 on your computer. It is off by default.

## Settings

Select the gear icon in the panel to:

- see the account that is connected, and sign out;
- choose the panel's language (it follows your browser by default);
- choose the resume used for the match;
- turn the on-page button on or off;
- open your browser's shortcut settings;
- turn desktop reminders on or off.

## Help

### A job site is not read correctly

Sites change their pages from time to time. If a field is empty or wrong:

1. Fix it in the panel before saving. Nothing is saved until you select **Save to Rezumi**.
2. If nothing could be read, select the description on the page and use the right-click menu, or paste the description into the panel.
3. Select **Report this page**. It sends the page address, the site, the extension version and which fields failed, never the page's content, and only after you select it. We use these reports to fix the readers.

<Tip>
  The panel can't read pages that need a sign-in the extension can't see, or that are not job postings. A posting you
  saved from its link in **Applications** keeps working there; see [Adding an
  application](/guides/adding-an-application).
</Tip>

### Why does the extension need LinkedIn access?

The extension asks for access to LinkedIn, Indeed, JobStreet and Glints (and rezumi.app) so it can show the **Save to Rezumi** button and the **Saved** mark on their job pages. On those sites it checks the address of the page you are on, inside your browser, to know which posting is open, and reads the posting only when you open the panel or press the button. It does not read profiles, messages or feeds, and it never presses a button for you.

Elsewhere it has no standing access: it reads a page only after you click the icon, use the shortcut or the right-click menu. See the [Privacy policy](/legal/privacy-policy) for how data is handled.

### How do I disconnect the extension?

- **Sign out in the extension:** open the panel's **Settings** and select **Sign out**. This ends the connection and removes its saved list from your browser.
- **End its access to your account:** in Rezumi, open **Settings**, then **Connected applications**, and select **Revoke access** next to the Rezumi browser extension. Do this too if you remove the extension from your browser, because removing it does not revoke access on the server.
- **Remove it:** in Chrome or Edge, open the extensions page, find Rezumi and select **Remove**.

Your saved applications stay in your account either way.
