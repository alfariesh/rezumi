You can choose how the app looks and which language its menus and buttons use. These settings change the app only. Your resumes always print on white paper, in the language set for each resume.

## Change the theme

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Select your name at the bottom of the sidebar, then **Settings**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick a theme under General">
    - **Light**: a light background all the time.
    - **Dark**: a dark background all the time.
    - **System**: follows your device's light or dark setting and switches when it does. This is the default.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The change applies at once. Rezumi remembers it in this browser, so another device or browser keeps its own choice.

### Another way to switch

Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> on Windows and Linux), choose **Change theme to…**, then pick a theme.

## Change the interface language

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Go to **Settings → General**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose a language under General">
    Open the picker and choose from more than 50 languages. You can type to filter the list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The page reloads in the new language. Like the theme, the choice is saved in this browser.

You can also use <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> and **Change language to…**.

The interface language doesn't translate your resume. Each resume has its own **Language** setting in the **Page** group of the editor's **Design** mode, which sets the section titles and date words on the page. See [Set the language](/guides/selecting-page-format#set-the-language).

## Set your timezone

Go to **Settings → General** and pick your timezone under **Timezone**. Interview times, reminders and schedules in Career use it. New accounts start on UTC; when your device is set to another timezone, **Use this device's timezone** switches to it in one step. The change is saved at once.

## Reduce motion

Rezumi has no motion switch of its own. It follows your operating system's reduced-motion setting: turn that on, and the app replaces movement with simple fades. Nothing in the app animates on its own without you doing something first.
