---
title: "Find roles while you are busy"
description: "Save a job search as a job alert, review the new openings on Home and track only the jobs you choose."
---

You have a job already and little time to scroll job boards. Save a search once as a **job alert**, then review new openings in **Home** when you have time.

A job alert repeats a search from [Jobs](/guides/searching-for-jobs) on a schedule. Each run looks only for jobs that appeared since the previous run, from the same job data providers as **Jobs**, so it needs no account or key. Openings you already have in **Home**, track in **Applications**, hid on **Jobs** or dismissed don't come back. The number of new roles varies; there is no promised daily count.

## Set what you are looking for

In **Profile → Preferences**, record target roles, places, minimum base salary, maximum office days and what matters to you. A new job alert starts from your first target role and place, and the match chips on each role compare it with these preferences.

**Where you're looking for work** sets the country a new search or alert starts with. It starts with the country of your account timezone, marked **From your timezone**. Select **Add a country** to choose another, or remove every country to search anywhere. **Use my timezone** returns to the automatic country.

Enter salary as a full amount, such as **95000**, rather than **95k**. Invalid shorthand stays visible until corrected. Choose **Any** for maximum office days when you have no requirement; it clears a previous choice.

Set your timezone in **Settings → General**. Alert times use that timezone.

## Save a search as a job alert

The quickest way is from **Jobs**:

1. Search for a role with the place and filters you want. See [Searching for jobs](/guides/searching-for-jobs).
2. Above the results, select **Get job alerts**.
3. Check the role, city and country. The search's other filters (work arrangement, level, minimum pay, visa sponsorship, posting window) are listed under **Also filtered by**.
4. Choose **Every day**, **Every 3 days** or **Every week**, then **First run** and **Time**. For morning results, choose a morning time such as 08:00.
5. Leave **Turn this schedule on** enabled and select **Save schedule**.

The button then reads **Job alert on** (or **Job alert paused**) whenever you open the same search, and selecting it opens that alert.

You can also start from **Home**: in **Schedules**, select **Add**, then **Job alert**. The role, city and country start from your preferences.

Job titles work best as the role, the same as on **Jobs**. The first run looks back one interval (a day for a daily alert, a week for a weekly one); later runs look for what appeared since the run before. Each run adds at most about 20 openings; when more appear, the newest come first.

How many job alerts can be on at once depends on your plan: **Free 1**, **Plus 3** and **Pro AI 10**. Paused alerts don't count. If you move to a plan with fewer alerts, your oldest alerts keep running and the others wait until you pause or delete some. Job alerts don't use your daily searches. See [Plans and payments](/guides/plans).

The server runs alerts while your browser is closed. See [Schedule controls](/guides/career-workspace-reference#schedules) for email options.

## Review the roles on Home

When an alert finds new openings, **Needs you** tells you there are roles to review. Under **New roles from your search**, each role shows the company and place, a summary, how it's worked and the listed pay when the posting gives them, and match details.

Read match chips as a starting point. **Salary not listed** or **Office days not stated** means the posting didn't say. It is a question to resolve, not confirmation that your preference is met.

For each role:

- **Read posting** opens the original posting to inspect the job before deciding.
- **Track in Applications** creates a Saved application with the role, company, place, listed pay, the posting and its link, the same as **Track** on **Jobs**. This does not submit an application.
- **Not for me** dismisses it. Use **Undo** if you change your mind.

A role whose posting is known to have closed leaves **New roles** on its own. Once tracked, [check Fit](/guides/checking-job-fit) using your experience, then prepare the application.

## Adjust or pause the alert

Select an alert's row in **Schedules** to change its role, place or frequency. To change its other filters, run the search again on **Jobs** with the new filters and save that as an alert. Use the row's switch to pause it without losing its settings. Delete it when you no longer need it; the notice offers **Undo**.

Pausing or changing an alert cancels its run still in progress. Changing the search starts over: its next run looks back one interval again.

If nothing new appears, check that the alert is on and when it runs next. A run can find no new openings, especially for narrow searches or small markets. When Rezumi limits job searching for the day, alert runs wait until the next day instead of failing. For a failure notice, see [troubleshooting](/guides/career-workspace-reference#when-something-does-not-work).
