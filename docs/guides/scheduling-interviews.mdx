title: "Scheduling interviews"
description: "Add interviews and follow-up reminders to your applications, see them on the Applications calendar, and add them to your own calendar with an .ics file."
---

Add each interview to its application so it shows as the application's next step, in its history and on the Applications calendar. You can also set a follow-up reminder, and send any dated step to the calendar app you already use.

## Schedule an interview

You can start from the application or from the calendar.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="From an application">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the application">
        Select it in **Applications** to open its details.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Choose Schedule an interview…">
        Under **Next step**, select **Edit**, then **Schedule an interview…**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Frame caption="The Next step Edit menu">
      <img src="/images/guides/scheduling-interviews/next-step-edit-menu.webp" alt="Next step card with the Edit menu open, listing Edit this interview…, Schedule an interview… and Set a follow-up…" />
    </Frame>

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="From the calendar">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the calendar">
        In **Applications**, select **All**, then select the calendar icon in the layout switch to the right of the search.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Start scheduling">
        Select **Schedule interview**. In **Day** or **Week**, you can also drag across empty hours to start at that time.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Choose the application">
        Under **Application**, pick the application the interview belongs to. Closed applications aren't listed.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

You can also use **Next step → Edit** in an application workspace. Then fill in the interview:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the interview type">
    Choose **Screening**, **Technical**, **Behavioral**, **Onsite** or **Other**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the date, time and timezone">
    Enter **Date** and **Time**. The timezone is shown beside Time and starts from your account's setting in **Settings
    → General**. If the invitation uses another timezone, select **Interview is in another timezone** and choose it.
    Check the equivalent time shown for your own timezone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the length">
    Under **Length · minutes**, choose 30, 45, 60 or 90. An existing interview with another length keeps that value
    available when you edit it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the meeting details">
    **Location** can hold a meeting link, address or phone number. Under **Who you'll meet**, use **Add a person** for
    each interviewer and add their name, role and optional public profile link. Copy the employer's request into **What
    they asked you to prepare**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">Select **Save interview**.</Step>
</Steps>

The interview appears in the application's **Activity**, on the calendar, and as the **Next step** until it has finished. Scheduling an interview does not change the application's stage; move it to **Interview** when appropriate.

## Edit or delete an interview

Select the interview on the calendar, in **Upcoming interviews**, or in the application's **Activity**. You can also use **Edit → Edit this interview…** in the details sheet, or **Next step → Edit** in its workspace. Change what you need and select **Save interview**.

A date and time skipped by a daylight-saving change is rejected. Pick a valid local time instead of accepting an automatic shift.

To remove it, select **Delete**. The notice offers **Undo** for six seconds. Undo keeps the original round and its linked schedules. Removing an interview pauses its briefing schedules; rescheduling moves them to the new time. Check **Home → Schedules** after a change.

## Prepare for the round

Open the application's workspace and choose **Prepare** for a timed plan, briefing and questions to ask. You can enable a morning briefing in advance, then use **Practice** to rehearse out loud. See [Get ready for interview day](/guides/preparing-for-interview-day).

## Use the calendar

Lay your applications out as a calendar (the calendar icon in the switch to the right of the search) to see interviews across all your applications. Rezumi remembers the layout on this device.

- Choose **Day**, **Week** or **Month**. Use the arrows to move back and forward, and **Today** to come back. The heading counts the interviews in the month on show.
- In **Month**, each day shows up to two interviews. Select **_n_ more** to see the rest of a busy day.
- Select an interview to edit it. In **Day** and **Week**, drag an interview to move it, or drag its edge to change its length.
- **Upcoming interviews**, on the right, lists every interview that hasn't finished yet, grouped by day. Select a card to edit the interview, or **View application** to open the application.
- The legend under the calendar shows the color of each interview type.

## Set a follow-up reminder

A follow-up is a reminder to check in, for example to email the recruiter a week after applying.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Set a follow-up…">
    In the application's details, under **Next step**, select **Edit**, then **Set a follow-up…** (or **Change the
    follow-up…**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a date and say what to do">
    Choose a **Date** and, if you like, fill in **What to do**, such as "Email the recruiter". When you change an
    existing follow-up, the fields start empty, so enter the date and note again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">Select **Save**. To remove the reminder, select **Clear**.</Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="The Follow-up dialog">
  <img
    src="/images/guides/scheduling-interviews/follow-up-dialog.webp"
    alt="Follow-up dialog with Date set to 03.10.2026, What to do set to Email the recruiter, and Clear and Save buttons"

The follow-up shows as the next step (unless an interview is coming up first) and turns amber once the date has passed. You can also set the follow-up date and note in **Edit details…**.

## Add a step to your calendar

When the next step is an interview or a follow-up, select **Add to calendar** under it. Rezumi downloads an `.ics` file named after the company and date. Open it to add the event to Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, Outlook or any other calendar app.

The event includes the type of step, the role and company, and the start and end time. Interviews also include where and your notes. Follow-ups are added as 30-minute events at 9:00 on the chosen day.

<Tip>
  The `.ics` file is a one-time copy. If you change the interview in Rezumi later, download it again and update the
  event in your calendar.
</Tip>
