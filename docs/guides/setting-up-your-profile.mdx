---
title: "Setting up your Profile"
description: "The first time you sign in, bring your LinkedIn profile, a resume file, your GitHub or your website, check what Rezumi found, and open your first resume a few minutes later."
---

The first time you sign in, Rezumi opens a short setup instead of Home. In four steps it fills your
[Profile](/guides/using-your-profile) from what you already have and makes your first resume from it. Every step is
optional: **Skip**, at the top right, takes you straight to Home.

## 1. Bring what you have

The first screen shows what your account already knows: the name, email and photo you signed up with. Below it, add any
of these, as many as you like:

- **LinkedIn profile link**: the address of your own public profile, for example `linkedin.com/in/your-name`. Tick
  **This is my own profile** to use it. You can read your own profile up to three times a day.
- **Resume file**: drop a PDF, Word file, LinkedIn data export (`.zip`) or a resume exported from Rezumi (JSON), or
  select **Choose**.
- **GitHub username**: adds your own public repositories as projects, and their languages as skills. If you signed in
  with GitHub, your username is already filled in.
- **Personal website**: Rezumi reads the page with AI and keeps only what is written there. On Rezumi AI this costs 2
  credits; with your own AI connection it costs none.

Select **Continue**. If you have nothing to add, select **Start from scratch**.

<Note>
  LinkedIn only shows when the site has LinkedIn reading turned on. If it isn't there, download your LinkedIn data
  export and add it as a resume file instead. See [Importing a resume](/guides/importing-resumes).
</Note>

Rezumi reads only what you add, and only when you select **Continue**. LinkedIn profiles are read through Apify and
GitHub accounts through GitHub, on Rezumi's behalf. Nothing is saved to your Profile until you check it in step 3.

## 2. Two quick questions

While your sources are read, Rezumi asks two questions. Both are optional:

- **What roles are you aiming for?** Type a role and press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to add it.
- **Where do you want to work?** Add cities or countries, then pick how many **office days a week** you want at most,
  from **Remote** to 5.

Your answers go to **Profile → Preferences**, where the coach and job matching use them. You can change them there at
any time.

Above the questions, each source shows its own progress. One that fails never stops the others:

- **Retry** appears when trying again can help, for example when a site took too long to answer.
- A link or username that can't be found shows what to check. Select **Back** to correct it.

Select **Review what we found** once your sources are in. You don't have to wait for the questions; you also don't have
to answer them.

## 3. Check what we found

Rezumi combines everything your sources found, by part: your details, the photo, the summary and each section, with a
count for each. When two sources have the same entry (the same company and position, for example), it appears once.
What your Profile already has isn't listed and doesn't change.

- Everything starts ticked. Untick an entry to leave it out, or untick a whole part with the box next to its name.
- If a photo was found, pick the one to use, or **No photo**. The photo you keep is copied into your account.

Select **Save to my Profile**. Only then are the ticked entries saved.

## 4. Pick a look for your first resume

The templates show your own details in each design. Select one, then **Create my resume**. The resume opens in the
editor, already filled from your Profile, and you can [change the template](/guides/choosing-a-template) at any time.
**More templates** shows the full gallery; templates outside your [plan](/guides/plans) are marked **Plus**.

Select **Not now** to go to Home without creating a resume.

## Skipping and coming back

**Skip** saves your Profile as it is (your name and email), so the setup doesn't open again. You can fill your Profile
later the same way: on **Profile → Details & career**, while it has no career entries yet, select **Bring what you
have**. See [Fill in your Profile](/guides/using-your-profile#fill-in-your-profile).

The setup only opens for an account whose Profile was never saved and that has no resumes or cover letters yet. Guest
accounts don't see it.
