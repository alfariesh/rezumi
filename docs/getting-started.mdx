Rezumi is a resume and cover-letter builder. You write your resume on one side of the screen and see the finished page on the other, then download it as a PDF or share it with a link. Start with Free. Plus ($4.99 a month) and Pro AI ($9.99 a month) are planned subscriptions, monthly or yearly, that you can cancel any time; paid plans and trial AI credits are coming soon while billing is disabled. See [plans](/use-cases/free-resume-builder) for launch limits.

[Create your free account](https://rezumi.app/auth/register) to build a resume and download it as PDF.
Want to look around first? [Browse resume templates](https://rezumi.app/resume-templates), including sample
PDF downloads, or [check a resume PDF without signing in](https://rezumi.app/ats-checker).

## What you can do with it

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Write resumes and cover letters" icon="file-lines">
    Keep every resume and letter in **Documents**. Start blank, from a sample, or by importing a PDF, Word file, JSON or
    LinkedIn export.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Design the page" icon="palette" href="https://rezumi.app/resume-templates">
    Pick from the template gallery, then adjust fonts, colors, page size and layout. The page updates as you type.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Check before you send" icon="list-check">
    **Check** mode points out issues on the lines they belong to and compares your resume with a job posting.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Share and download" icon="share-nodes">
    Download a PDF, Word, OpenDocument, Markdown or JSON file, or publish a link that you can protect with a password.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Track job applications" icon="briefcase">
    Follow each application from saved to offer in a list, board or calendar, and see what's working in Insights.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Get help from an assistant" icon="sparkles">
    Connect your own AI provider, and the assistant proposes edits you accept or reject one by one.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Who it's for

Rezumi is for anyone applying for jobs. You don't need design skills or an account with any other service.
The app is available in 55 languages, works on phones, tablets and computers, and has light and dark themes.

Use [rezumi.app](https://rezumi.app/auth/register) to create a free account and start writing.

## Your data

Your resumes belong to you. You can download any document as JSON and import it back later, export everything in your
account from **Settings**, and delete your account at any time. See the [Privacy policy](/legal/kebijakan-privasi) for data handling and rights.
