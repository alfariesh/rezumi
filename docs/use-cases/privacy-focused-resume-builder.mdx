Rezumi keeps documents private until you choose to share them. AI sends the content needed for the operation to the selected provider;
review the provider and sharing prompt before use. See the [Privacy policy](/legal/kebijakan-privasi).

## Private by default

- **Documents stay in your account.** Nobody else can see your resumes or cover letters. A resume becomes visible only when you publish it in **Share**. Cover letters have no public link.
- **Search engines are your choice.** Web pages ask search engines not to list them unless you turn on **Show in search engines** for that resume.
- **Contact details can stay hidden.** Hide your email, phone and location from the page, its PDF and search results.
- **Links can require a password.** Add one in Share, and turn the link off at any time to stop access immediately.
- **Private notes stay private.** Notes you keep on a resume never appear on the page, in the PDF or on the public link. Hidden sections and entries are removed from the public link too.

## Checks that never leave your browser

The public [ATS checker](/guides/using-the-ats-checker) reads your PDF in your browser. The file is not uploaded and you do not need an account. In the editor, Check mode's score, issues and job match also run without sending your resume to an AI service.

## AI choices

Your own provider key works on every plan. Pro AI also includes Rezumi AI credits, so you can use AI without a key of your own.
AI processing follows the selected provider’s terms and retention; inspect the data being shared before proceeding.
You can revoke connected providers and applications from settings.

## Account security and your data

Protect your account with [two-factor authentication](/guides/setting-up-two-factor-authentication) or [passkeys](/guides/setting-up-passkeys). You can [export all your data](/guides/exporting-your-data) as a zip at any time, and deleting your account permanently removes your documents and applications.
