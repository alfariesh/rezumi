Rezumi has a free plan and planned monthly or yearly subscriptions. **Plus, Pro AI and trial credits are coming soon while billing is disabled**;
paid-plan limits are not active yet. Prices and availability appear on the [homepage](https://rezumi.app/#pricing).

Application tracking, insights, knowledge, ATS checks, sharing, sync and version history are on every plan.
Your own AI key works on every plan, billed separately by your provider. Trial credits start on first Rezumi AI use after email verification.

Paid access begins after verified payment and renews every month or year until you cancel. Cancel any time and keep access until the period you paid for ends; upgrades apply at once with the difference charged, downgrades at the next renewal. Prices are before tax: Polar, the merchant of record, adds the tax for where you live at checkout and handles payment and invoices. A first Plus subscription starts with 7 free days; a card is taken at checkout but nothing is charged until the trial ends, and cancelling during the trial costs nothing.
Documents stay editable, shareable and downloadable as PDF after a plan ends. Free limits only restrict creating more documents.
Credits do not roll over; failed operations without usable results return credits. Costs are shown before each action.
