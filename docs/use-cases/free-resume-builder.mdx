Rezumi has a free plan and two planned paid plans: monthly or yearly card subscriptions, or, from Indonesia, 30-day and 365-day
plans paid with QRIS. **Plus, Pro AI and trial credits are coming soon while billing is disabled**;
paid-plan limits are not active yet. Prices and availability appear on the [homepage](https://rezumi.app/#pricing).

Application tracking, insights, knowledge, ATS checks, sharing, sync and version history are on every plan.
Your own AI key works on every plan, billed separately by your provider. Trial credits start on first Rezumi AI use after email verification.

Paid access begins after verified payment. A card subscription renews every month or year until you cancel. Cancel any time and keep access until the period you paid for ends; upgrades apply at once with the difference charged, downgrades at the next renewal. Card prices are before tax: Dodo Payments, the merchant of record, adds the tax for where you live at checkout and handles payment and invoices. A first Plus subscription by card starts with 7 free days; a card is taken at checkout but nothing is charged until the trial ends, and cancelling during the trial costs nothing.

From Indonesia you can pay in Rupiah with QRIS from any bank or e-wallet app instead. A QRIS plan is sold by Rezumi's operator at a final price with no tax added, lasts 30 or 365 days and doesn't renew: nothing is charged later, and you get an email 3 days before it ends. Paying again adds the time after your current plan; moving up to Pro AI starts it at once, and the rest of Plus continues after it. QRIS plans have no free trial.

Documents stay editable, shareable and downloadable as PDF after a plan ends. Free limits only restrict creating more documents.
Credits do not roll over; failed operations without usable results return credits. Costs are shown before each action.
